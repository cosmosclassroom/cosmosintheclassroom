Our Three Service Pathways

1
Early Inquiries

Strategic AI & Institutional Advising

Dedicated monthly counsel to guide your AI strategy, policy development, and team readiness.

Learn More +

This is a long-term, high-level partnership for leaders navigating the future. We provide dedicated monthly hours to provide technological and pedagogical counsel as you develop your district's AI strategies and policies.

This pathway is for you if:

  • You are developing district-wide AI policy and ethics guidelines.
  • You need to model new strategic initiatives or track their progress.
  • You need consistent, high-level support for your leadership and curriculum teams.
  • You have a small team who wants to integrate AI into their workflows and want guidance.

Deliverables:

  • Ongoing strategic counsel and leadership workshops.
  • Policy drafting, analysis, and refinement.
  • Clear, consistent communication drafting for internal stakeholders.
2
Recommended starting point

The DNA Discovery Audit

Focused analysis of a single domain to reveal strengths, gaps, and opportunities.

Learn More +

This is a focused, high-value, one-time project. It's a low-risk starting point to diagnose a specific challenge or domain. We read the complete source "DNA" of a single domain—your as-is curriculum, your policies, your resource libraries—to provide a comprehensive analysis.

This pathway is for you if:

  • You know a specific domain (e.g., "9th Grade ELA," "District AI Policy," or "Student Support Pathways") has alignment or consistency issues, but you can't pinpoint why.
  • You need to see a clear map of a domain's strengths, gaps, redundancies, and alignment blind spots.
  • You want a tangible, data-driven report to build a case for a larger initiative.

Deliverables:

  • A complete "DNA Map" of the target domain.
  • A comprehensive Gap & Connection Analysis Report.
  • A strategic proposal and action plan for a full Custom Codex Implementation.
3
Ready to Begin

The Custom Codex Implementation

Flagship service: design, configure, and launch your private AI assistant.

Learn More +

This is our flagship service. We move from analysis to action. This engagement includes the full DNA Audit (Service 2) and results in the design, configuration, and launch of your private, stakeholder-specific Codex.

We partner with your team to create an iterative AI partner that handles the "machine work" of alignment, recall, and drafting, freeing your staff to focus on their core mission.

This pathway is for you if:

  • You are ready to reduce burnout and innovate for a specific team.
  • You want to preserve the expertise of your veteran staff and unify a team's approach.
  • You want to provide your teams with a "Codex" that enables their creativity and mastery while automating cohesion and alignment.

Deliverables:

  • All deliverables from the DNA Discovery Audit.
  • A custom-configured "Codex" for your target stakeholders (e.g., "Mission-Anchored," "Pathways & Resources," or "Course Sculptor").
  • Full team training, onboarding, iterating, and support.

Our Implementation Process

When you engage us for the Custom Codex Implementation, here's exactly what happens:

1

Institutional DNA Sequencing

We "read" your source documents (e.g., curriculum, policy manuals, resource libraries) to map the unique DNA of your institution.

2

Gap & Connection Analysis

Your team receives a detailed "DNA map" identifying narrative gaps, redundancies, and alignment opportunities.

3

Custom Codex Training

We train your Codex on your specific patterns, ensuring all generated content maintains your unique voice and standards.

4

Team Integration

Your team receives hands-on training to effectively collaborate with the Codex, focusing on your specific use cases and workflows.

5

Iterative Refinement

Regular check-ins and adjustments ensure the Codex evolves with your team's needs and maintains perfect alignment with your goals.
