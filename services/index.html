Preserve Your Wisdom, Honor Your Teachers

We encode your school's unique instructional legacy and relieve your teachers from digital tedium.

We do this by helping you codify your curriculum's "DNA" into a private, iterative AI partner called a Codex. This allows your team to focus on the essential human work of pedagogical mastery, creative design, and strategic leadership.

We can then customize an AI environment that offers your team a space to iterate, create, and innovate while remaining cohesive and aligned with colleagues.

Human teachers help make it viable. Machines help guarantee alignment.

We partner with you in three distinct ways.

1. Strategic AI & Curriculum Advising

This is a long-term, high-level advising partnership for leaders in the nascent stages of AI integration. We provide dedicated monthly hours to provide technological and pedagogical counsel as you develop your district's AI strategies and policies.

This pathway is for you if:

  • You are developing district-wide AI policy and ethics guidelines.
  • You need to model new strategic initiatives or track their progress.
  • You need consistent, high-level support for your curriculum teams.
  • You have a small team of teachers who want to integrate AI into their workflows and want guidance.

Deliverables:

  • Ongoing strategic counsel and leadership workshops.
  • Policy drafting, analysis, and refinement.
  • Clear, consistent communication drafting for internal stakeholders.

2. The Single-Course DNA Audit

This is a focused, high-value, one-time project. It's a low-risk starting point to diagnose a specific challenge or course. We read the complete source "DNA" of a single course—your as-is curriculum, standards, and expert teacher wisdom—to provide a comprehensive analysis.

This pathway is for you if:

  • You know a specific course (e.g., 9th Grade ELA) has alignment or consistency issues, but you can't pinpoint why.
  • You need to see a clear map of a course's strengths, gaps, redundancies, and vertical alignment blind spots.
  • You want a tangible, data-driven report to build a case for a larger curriculum initiative.

Deliverables:

  • A complete "DNA Map" of the target course.
  • A comprehensive Gap & Connection Analysis Report.
  • A strategic proposal and action plan for a full Custom Codex Implementation.

3. The Custom Codex Implementation

This is our flagship service. We move from analysis to action. This engagement includes the full DNA Audit (Service 2) and results in the design, configuration, and launch of your private, custom Codex.

We partner with your team to create an iterative AI partner that handles the "machine work" of alignment, recall, and drafting, freeing your teachers to sculpt their courses with pedagogical mastery.

This pathway is for you if:

  • You are ready to reduce burnout and innovate content for a specific course or team.
  • You want to preserve the expertise of your veteran teachers and unify a course pedagogically.
  • You want to provide your teachers with a "Course Sculptor" Codex that enables their creativity and mastery while automating cohesion and alignment.

Deliverables:

  • All deliverables from the Single-Course DNA Audit.
  • A custom-configured "Course Sculptor" Codex for teachers.
  • A custom-configured "Strategic Alignment" Codex for curriculum leads and administrators.
  • Full team training, onboarding, iterating, and support.

Our Implementation Process

When you engage us for the Custom Codex Implementation, here's exactly what happens:

1

Curriculum DNA Sequencing

We "read" your GVC, standards, and resource library to map the unique DNA of your curriculum. This creates the foundation for your custom Codex.

2

Gap & Connection Analysis

Your curriculum team receives a detailed "DNA map" identifying narrative gaps, redundancies, and alignment opportunities within your content.

3

Custom Codex Training

We train your Codex on your specific curriculum patterns, ensuring all generated content maintains your unique voice and standards.

4

Team Integration

Your team receives hands-on training to effectively collaborate with the Codex, focusing on your specific use cases and workflows.

5

Iterative Refinement

Regular check-ins and adjustments ensure the Codex evolves with your team's needs and maintains perfect alignment with your standards.

Our Non-Negotiable Commitment: Privacy First

We never store student PII. We never evaluate teachers. Our process is designed to support, analyze, and generate curriculum content, not to monitor humans.
