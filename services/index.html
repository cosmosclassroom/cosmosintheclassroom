Our Implementation Process

When you engage us for the Custom Codex Implementation, here's exactly what happens:

1

Curriculum DNA Sequencing

We "read" your existing curriculum—standards frameworks, resource libraries, and even "old" content that veteran teachers have pocketed away. Everything matters. This creates the foundation for your custom Codex.

2

Faculty Wisdom Capture

We help the department or curriculum team define their voice, their role, and their desired student and teacher experiences. We merge the experience and wisdom of the entire department into a queryable Codex—preserving institutional knowledge that would otherwise be lost when veteran teachers retire.

3

Gap & Connection Analysis

We weave together your "prix-fixe" curriculum (purchased programs, textbooks, platforms) with your faculty's expertise, experience, and best practices. The result is a unified "DNA map" identifying narrative gaps, redundancies, and alignment opportunities with tried-and-true legacy content.

4

Custom Codex Training

We train teachers how to take the core curriculum DNA and sculpt it into customized lessons they'll actually use—because they design them. The Codex handles alignment and institutional consistency while teachers exercise creative control over pacing, context, and pedagogy.

5

Team Integration

We work directly with your teachers to integrate the Codex into their actual workflows. This means understanding how they plan units, design assessments, and respond to student needs in real-time—then showing them how the Codex amplifies (not replaces) their expertise. Teachers learn to query their department's collective wisdom, adapt legacy content to new contexts, and generate scaffolded materials that maintain institutional voice while serving their unique classroom needs.

6

Iterative Refinement

We utilize AI to track changes teacher-to-teacher and year-to-year, ensuring that teachers feel free to experiment while maintaining institutional coherence. This shifts your curriculum from changing to adapting with intention. The Codex documents what works, preserves innovations, and helps new teachers benefit from the collective wisdom of those who came before.

Our Non-Negotiable Commitment: Privacy First

We never store student PII. We never evaluate teachers. Our process is designed to support, analyze, and generate curriculum content, not to monitor humans.
