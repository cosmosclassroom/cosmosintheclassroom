The Custom Codex in Action

Real solutions for the people who make education happen

The Custom Codex is an iterative AI partner configured from your unique "curriculum DNA"—your mission, standards, and the collective wisdom of your expert teachers.

It handles the computational work of alignment, analysis, and recall, freeing your team for the essential human work of pedagogical mastery, creative design, and strategic leadership.

Here's how it serves each role in your organization:

Jump to your role:

Administrators Curriculum Teams Teachers Support Teams
Administrators

The Mission-Anchored Codex

Ensure every decision reflects your core mission. The Codex handles policy modeling and alignment analysis, freeing you to focus on strategic leadership.

Privacy guarantee: Never used for teacher evaluation.

Mission-Aligned Policy

The Challenge

New policies are drafted quickly, but anchoring them to your district's mission takes time you don't have.

How the Codex Helps

Train it on your mission statement. Then iterate: "Analyze this policy draft against our mission—where's the misalignment?" or "Draft three options for [issue] that explicitly reflect our 'whole-child' approach."

The Result

Mission-anchored leadership. Cross-check every policy decision against your values in seconds, not hours.

Initiative Modeling

The Challenge

You're piloting a new strategic initiative, but modeling its impact and securing buy-in is complex.

How the Codex Helps

It generates alignment reports and stakeholder communications. You iterate: "What are the three biggest hurdles for the ELA department?" "Draft a one-page parent summary aligned with our mission."

The Result

Data-driven planning. Model impact, generate targeted communications, and plan smarter rollouts.

Progress Tracking

The Challenge

Multi-phase initiatives scatter updates across documents and emails, making progress tracking painful.

How the Codex Helps

Configure it with initiative goals and phases. Ask: "Summarize Phase 2 progress" or "Which teams are behind on the PBL rollout?" "Generate a board report."

The Result

Institutional memory. Comprehensive oversight without email archaeology.

Curriculum Teams

The Cohesion & Creativity Codex

Balance creative energy with curricular coherence. The Codex serves as your institutional memory and alignment partner across departments and grade levels.

Cross-Disciplinary Connections

The Challenge

Departments work in silos, missing powerful thematic connections between subjects.

How the Codex Helps

After reading multiple curricula, query it: "Where do 10th Grade World History and ELA overlap thematically?" "Generate a cross-curricular project idea."

The Result

Breaking silos. Reveal hidden connections that enable powerful collaborations.

Long-Term Revision Cycles

The Challenge

Months between curriculum sessions mean days lost reconstructing context and decisions.

How the Codex Helps

Ask: "Summarize the changelog for Unit 3" or "Where did we leave off on 7th-grade vertical alignment? What are the unresolved issues?"

The Result

Perfect institutional memory. Resume productive work immediately without hours of reconstruction.

Vertical Alignment Mapping

The Challenge

Tracking how skills scaffold across K-12 is nearly impossible manually.

How the Codex Helps

Query the full scope: "Show the complete scaffold for argumentative writing from 6-12" or "Generate a gap report between 5th and 6th grade math."

The Result

Clear vertical mapping. See the complete picture, identify gaps, and make strategic scope decisions.

Teachers

The "Course Sculptor" Codex

Your private co-planner that provides the standards and alignment foundation, freeing you to sculpt courses with your unique pedagogical style.

Privacy guarantee: Never evaluates you—only serves you.

Course Design Freedom

The Challenge

You want to design a course reflecting your teaching style, but balancing creativity with alignment is exhausting.

How the Codex Helps

It handles the alignment "scaffolding." You sculpt and iterate: "Draft a lesson on [topic] using inquiry-based methods" or "Check this project for standards alignment."

The Result

Artistry, not compliance. Design the course you've envisioned without drowning in paperwork.

Collaborative Lesson Design

The Challenge

Your PLC has great ideas but gets stuck in "blank page" paralysis and alignment details.

How the Codex Helps

Provide a creative brief. The Codex generates an aligned skeleton. Iterate live: "What's a different phenomenon we could use?" "Generate three assessment options."

The Result

Creativity on a foundation. Spend PLC time on pedagogy, not standards wrestling.

Rapid Differentiation

The Challenge

Adapting resources for multiple reading levels or ELL students takes hours you don't have.

How the Codex Helps

Define the need: "Simplify this text for 9th grade" or "Translate this to Spanish while maintaining academic rigor." Iterate as needed.

The Result

Differentiation without the grind. Create multiple versions in minutes, not hours.

Support Teams

The Consistency & Support Codex

You are the critical support network for the entire school community. Your goal is to provide consistent, accurate support to students, teachers, and families, ensuring everyone can find the right information—from health policies to curriculum resources. The Codex acts as your central, verifiable knowledge base.

Siloed Policy & Protocols

The Challenge

Policy documents are scattered across drives. A nurse needs the medication administration protocol. A counselor needs the wellness referral steps. Each search wastes time and risks using outdated information.

How the Codex Helps

Configure it with all district handbooks and protocols. Ask: "What is the official policy for administering medication?" or "Summarize the 3-step protocol for a student wellness referral."

The Result

Verifiable single source of truth. Stop hunting for "the right document." Provide consistent, official answers to parents and staff, reducing liability and confusion.

Misaligned Academic Support

The Challenge

A librarian needs resources for a unit but doesn't know the learning goals. A paraprofessional wants to help but isn't sure what aligned questions to ask. Support work happens in a vacuum.

How the Codex Helps

Knowing your GVC and curriculum, it answers: "Show me the key themes for 10th Grade ELA Unit 3" or "Generate 3 aligned exit ticket questions for 7th-grade math."

The Result

Aligned, effective intervention. Support staff become force-multipliers for teachers. Their work—recommendations, small-group help—is now directly aligned with the official curriculum.

New Hire Onboarding

The Challenge

New counselors, paras, and support staff are overwhelmed trying to find official procedures, contacts, and protocols. Veteran staff spend hours answering the same orientation questions.

How the Codex Helps

New hires ask: "Summarize the 504 accommodation process" or "Who is the district contact for special education?" "Where do I find my duty schedule?"

The Result

Accelerated onboarding. New staff find official answers in moments, not days. They feel competent faster and become self-sufficient, reducing the training burden on veteran staff.
