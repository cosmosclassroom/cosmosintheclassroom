Our Implementation Process

1

Curriculum DNA Sequencing

We "read" your GVC, standards, and resource library to map the unique DNA of your curriculum. This creates the foundation for your custom Gem.

2

Gap & Connection Analysis

Your curriculum team receives a detailed "DNA map" identifying narrative gaps, redundancies, and alignment opportunities within your content.

3

Custom Gem Training

We train your Gem on your specific curriculum patterns, ensuring all generated content maintains your unique voice and standards.

4

Team Integration

Your team receives hands-on training to effectively collaborate with the Gem, focusing on your specific use cases and workflows.

5

Iterative Refinement

Regular check-ins and adjustments ensure the Gem evolves with your team's needs and maintains perfect alignment with your standards.
