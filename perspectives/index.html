Perspectives

Essays on the intersection of artificial intelligence, teaching expertise, and institutional memory.

Michel de Montaigne introduced the essay—literally "the attempt"—as a form of exploratory writing.

These essays are attempts to consider the tensions and connections between human wisdom and machine intelligence, between institutional stability and creative adaptation, between the work we ask teachers to do and the work they do best.

They are explorations of how AI might help us preserve what matters in education—not by replacing teachers, but by returning to them their craft.
