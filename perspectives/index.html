Perspectives

Essays on the intersection of artificial intelligence, teaching expertise, and institutional memory.

Michel de Montaigne introduced the essay—literally "the attempt"—as a form of exploratory writing. Not declarations, not manifestos, but honest attempts to think through complex problems.

These essays are attempts to understand the tensions between human wisdom and machine intelligence, between institutional stability and creative adaptation, between the work we ask teachers to do and the work they were called to do.

They are explorations of how AI might help us preserve what matters in education—not by replacing teachers, but by finally allowing them to teach.
