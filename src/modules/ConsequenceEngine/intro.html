Welcome, City Planner!

Your community is counting on you.

The Game's Objective

In this simulation, you are responsible for managing a community's resilience and public trust. A disaster has struck, and your job is to propose solutions to mitigate the damage and strengthen the city against future events.

Your final score will be a combination of how well your solutions improve both these variables:

  • Resilience: The physical ability of your town to withstand and recover from a disaster. This includes structural integrity, operational functionality, and resource availability.
  • Trust: The faith your citizens have in their leaders and institutions. A high trust score means citizens feel safe, informed, and confident in the community's future.

How to Play

The game follows a simple three-step process:

  1. Choose Your Town: Select a community and read about its unique characteristics and vulnerabilities.
  2. Face a Disaster: A random disaster will occur, impacting your town's resilience and trust scores.
  3. Propose Solutions: You will be presented with a list of solutions. Each solution has a **cost** and will improve your town's scores in different ways. Choose wisely to rebuild your community.

Be aware of **random modifiers**! After you propose a solution, a chance event might occur—like a labor strike or an unexpected state investment—that will alter the final outcome of your choice.

Your Roles

You will act as a combination of three critical roles to make your decisions:

The Scientist

Analyzes data and understands the fundamental forces at play, predicting potential disasters.

The Engineer

Translates scientific data into practical design solutions and builds resilient infrastructure.

The Policymaker

Creates and implements plans and campaigns to protect the public and rebuild community trust.
