Disaster Scenarios

Each scenario presents a unique challenge for your community. Your team must choose solutions to overcome the **Cost of Inaction**, which is the negative combined score of Resilience and Community Trust.

Earthquakes

The Midtown Fault Line

A previously unknown fault line has shifted beneath the city's financial district. The earthquake causes significant damage to skyscrapers and key data centers, but the ground remains mostly intact. Panic spreads as the city's economic heart grinds to a halt.

Havenwood

Havenwood's downtown lacks modern seismic codes, and its economic district relies on older, rigid structures that are highly susceptible to damage from this kind of quake.

Total: -14.6 Resilience: -9.0 Trust: -5.6

Port St. Jude

Due to its role as a major financial hub, Port St. Jude's downtown is densely packed with tall, vulnerable buildings and critical data infrastructure that are at high risk from this inland fault line event.

Total: -16.8 Resilience: -12.0 Trust: -4.8

Whispering Pines

While its buildings are lower, Whispering Pines' remote location means a longer emergency response time. The city's reliance on a single major access road makes it difficult to bring in aid when the road is impassable.

Total: -19.0 Resilience: -11.0 Trust: -8.0

The Liquefaction Event

A moderate earthquake hits the city's coastal industrial zone. Buildings remain largely upright, but the ground turns to a liquid-like substance. Roads, pipelines, and train tracks buckle and submerge, cutting off key supply lines and utility services.

Havenwood

As an inland town, Havenwood's infrastructure is largely unaffected by the liquefaction near the coast, but it still suffers from supply chain disruptions due to damaged external transit routes.

Total: -12.8 Resilience: -7.2 Trust: -5.6

Port St. Jude

Port St. Jude's heavy industrial port and extensive coastal infrastructure are built on reclaimed land, making them extremely susceptible to liquefaction. This event severely impacts their logistics and economy.

Total: -14.4 Resilience: -9.6 Trust: -4.8

Whispering Pines

Being a mountain town, Whispering Pines has no risk of liquefaction. Its primary impact comes from disrupted goods coming up from the coast, causing short-term shortages and a loss of public trust.

Total: -16.8 Resilience: -8.8 Trust: -8.0

Tsunamis & Flooding

The Upriver Surge

An offshore earthquake sends a small tsunami into a river's estuary, where it is funneled inland. The town, located on the river's upper banks, is unprepared for the sudden surge of water, which overwhelms its outdated flood defenses and causes flash flooding.

Havenwood

Located on the banks of a major river, Havenwood is directly in the path of the upriver surge. Its older flood protection systems are not equipped to handle the rapid-onset flash flood, leading to significant damage.

Total: -13.3 Resilience: -5.6 Trust: -7.7

Port St. Jude

As a coastal port city with significant tsunami defenses, Port St. Jude is more resilient to a sea-based surge. However, its expansive, low-lying coastal areas still suffer damage, and residents lose trust in the city's ability to protect them from the unexpected.

Total: -14.0 Resilience: -9.1 Trust: -4.9

Whispering Pines

Whispering Pines, being inland and at a high elevation, is not affected by the upriver surge. Their score reflects a minor impact from supply chain issues, but no direct damage.

Total: -11.2 Resilience: -4.9 Trust: -6.3

The Hundred-Year Flood

Following a week of sustained rainfall, the main river crests, overtopping its banks and flooding the entire downtown area. The event was predicted, but a lack of public funds for levee maintenance leads to catastrophic failure.

Havenwood

Havenwood's downtown area is centered around the river and is highly vulnerable to this type of large-scale flooding. The event was predicted, and the public's loss of trust is a major component of its negative score.

Total: -18.2 Resilience: -7.2 Trust: -11.0

Port St. Jude

The city's extensive, low-lying industrial parks and public infrastructure are exposed to this large-scale flooding. The lack of proper maintenance on flood defenses is a major factor in the high resilience score.

Total: -18.7 Resilience: -11.7 Trust: -7.0

Whispering Pines

Due to its high elevation, Whispering Pines is less exposed to a river-based flood. However, the heavy rainfall still leads to localized flash flooding and mudslides, resulting in significant disruption to daily life and loss of public trust.

Total: -15.3 Resilience: -6.3 Trust: -9.0

Fires

The WUI Fire

A fire ignites in the national forest near the Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI), the zone where the forest meets a new suburb. The fire spreads quickly, consuming homes that were built without fire-resistant materials and threatening the lives of residents who are unprepared for evacuation.

Havenwood

Havenwood's outer suburbs are built on the edge of a large, dry forest, making it highly susceptible to a WUI fire. The lack of proper fire-resistant building codes and evacuation plans is reflected in the high resilience score.

Total: -14.5 Resilience: -10.0 Trust: -4.5

Port St. Jude

Port St. Jude's sprawling suburban areas are at the highest risk from a WUI fire. Many newly constructed homes have been built with minimal attention to fire safety, creating a highly vulnerable population and infrastructure.

Total: -17.0 Resilience: -11.0 Trust: -6.0

Whispering Pines

As a town located in a national forest, Whispering Pines has the most experience with wildfires. They are not as susceptible as the other towns because of their "defensible space" programs and early warning systems.

Total: -11.5 Resilience: -6.0 Trust: -5.5

The Industrial Park Fire

A massive fire breaks out in a chemical storage facility at the city's industrial park. The blaze threatens to spread to an adjacent neighborhood and releases toxic smoke into the air. The fire department is overwhelmed, and public health officials issue a dire warning.

Havenwood

Havenwood has a large industrial park, which makes it a high risk for this type of event. It also has a densely populated residential area adjacent to the industrial park, increasing the risk of harm to the population.

Total: -16.1 Resilience: -8.0 Trust: -8.1

Port St. Jude

Port St. Jude is home to a major port and many chemical storage facilities, making it the most vulnerable city to a fire of this nature. The toxic smoke would also have an outsized impact on the large population living near the port.

Total: -19.6 Resilience: -8.8 Trust: -10.8

Whispering Pines

As a small, inland town with no major industrial sites, Whispering Pines has the lowest risk of a fire of this nature. The high negative trust score reflects the difficulty of getting help to the remote town.

Total: -14.7 Resilience: -4.8 Trust: -9.9

Coastal Storms

Chronic Erosion

The town's primary beach, a major tourism draw, has been slowly eroding over the last decade. A popular coastal highway is now just a few feet from the water's edge. Experts warn that a single major storm could destroy the highway and devastate the local economy.

Havenwood

Although inland, Havenwood relies heavily on its river for commerce and transportation, making it vulnerable to the downstream effects of chronic erosion. The local economy is not well-diversified and a coastal disaster would have a ripple effect.

Total: -14.4 Resilience: -5.6 Trust: -8.8

Port St. Jude

Port St. Jude is a coastal city with a major tourism economy that is largely dependent on its beaches. It is also more susceptible to coastal erosion, so the city faces the highest risk from this disaster.

Total: -14.0 Resilience: -8.4 Trust: -5.6

Whispering Pines

As a small inland town, Whispering Pines has the lowest risk of a coastal storm. Their resilience to this type of event is high, and the negative score reflects a minor impact from supply chain issues.

Total: -11.3 Resilience: -4.9 Trust: -6.4

The Hurricane

A powerful Category 4 hurricane makes a direct landfall. The storm's high winds tear down power lines, and its torrential rain causes widespread flooding. The entire region is left without power for a week, and essential services are cut off.

Havenwood

Havenwood, though inland, is situated on a large river, which floods from the massive rainfall. The town's grid is also vulnerable to high winds, making it highly susceptible to a major power outage.

Total: -18.7 Resilience: -8.8 Trust: -9.9

Port St. Jude

As a coastal city, Port St. Jude is directly impacted by the storm surge, high winds, and flooding. The city's aging infrastructure and heavy reliance on the port for its economy make it the most vulnerable city to a hurricane.

Total: -19.5 Resilience: -13.2 Trust: -6.3

Whispering Pines

The inland location of Whispering Pines protects it from the worst effects of a hurricane. However, the town's power grid is still susceptible to high winds, and its remote location can cause problems for relief efforts.

Total: -14.9 Resilience: -7.7 Trust: -7.2
