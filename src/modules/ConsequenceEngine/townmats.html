Havenwood

The Legacy Community

Havenwood is a town with deep historical roots and a close-knit population. Many residents trust in traditional knowledge and their neighbors more than they trust outside experts or government intervention. The town's infrastructure is old, but so is its history of overcoming challenges through self-reliance. Your challenge is to navigate these deep-seated beliefs and physical vulnerabilities to protect a town that values its past above all else.

Rationale Summary

Earthquake: Old, un-retrofitted buildings and resistance to costly, mandated upgrades.

Tsunami/Flooding: Unmaintained river embankments, but strong community cohesion.

Fire: Low-risk geography leads to a lack of awareness and investment.

Coastal Storms: Vulnerable embankments, but a history of survival builds trust.

Port St. Jude

The Economic Engine

Port St. Jude is a bustling and diverse coastal metropolis. Its high-tech port and thriving economy give it a large tax base, allowing for ambitious infrastructure projects and rapid response. However, the dense and transient population makes it difficult to build a unified sense of community, and the city's diverse interests often lead to political gridlock. Your challenge is to unite a divided populace and spend its wealth wisely to ensure its future.

Rationale Summary

Earthquake: Modern codes, but urban density and competing interests lead to low trust.

Tsunami/Flooding: High tax base for investment, but urban planning is contentious.

Fire: High density acts as a fire break, and a proactive government builds high trust.

Coastal Storms: Public funds are available, but a transient population creates low trust.

Whispering Pines

The Expanding Suburb

Whispering Pines is a new, rapidly growing suburb built on a scenic bluff overlooking the coast and a forested estuary. The community is full of young families who have chosen the area for its natural beauty. While they have a strong sense of collective purpose, they are new to the area's natural risks and are unprepared for the unique challenges of living at the intersection of land and sea.

Tsunami/Flooding: Vulnerable bluffs and resident resistance to policies that affect property values.

Fire: High vulnerability in the WUI, but proactive homeowners support mitigation efforts.

Coastal Storms: New homes are on vulnerable land and residents' trust is untested by a major storm.
