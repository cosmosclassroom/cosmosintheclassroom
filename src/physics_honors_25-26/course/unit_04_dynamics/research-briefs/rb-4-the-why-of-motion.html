Assessment Criteria

Forecast (25 points)

  • Initial Model (10 points): Clear, detailed prediction with logical reasoning
  • Variable Identification (8 points): Identifies relevant variables with thoughtful predictions
  • Prior Knowledge (4 points): Makes appropriate connections to previous learning
  • Research Questions (3 points): Poses investigable questions

Blueprint (35 points)

  • Model Refinement (10 points): Shows clear evolution in thinking based on evidence
  • Mathematical Relationships (10 points): Accurately identifies and expresses relationships
  • Scientific Explanation (10 points): Provides mechanism-based explanation using scientific principles
  • Application (5 points): Thoughtful application to practical situations

Reflection (40 points)

  • Final Model (12 points): Comprehensive, accurate, well-integrated understanding
  • Evidence Evaluation (8 points): Thoughtful analysis of evidence quality and support
  • Limitations (6 points): Honest assessment of model limitations and uncertainties
  • Real-World Connections (6 points): Meaningful connections to applications
  • Metacognition (4 points): Thoughtful reflection on learning process
  • Future Questions (4 points): Generates meaningful questions for further investigation

Quality Indicators Throughout

  • Uses appropriate scientific vocabulary
  • Shows proper units and significant figures in calculations
  • Demonstrates understanding of unit learning objectives
  • Applies thinking lens consistently and meaningfully
  • Shows evidence of intellectual growth and curiosity

Instructor Notes

Timing and Pacing

  • Forecast: Complete during first 2-3 days of unit
  • Blueprint: Complete after initial investigations, typically mid-unit
  • Reflection: Complete during final 2-3 days of unit

Differentiation Strategies

  • Provide sentence starters for students who need additional structure
  • Offer choice in how students represent their models (drawings, diagrams, written descriptions)
  • Allow collaborative discussion before individual completion
  • Provide additional scaffolding prompts for complex reasoning tasks

Integration with Unit Activities

  • Reference specific hexagon labs and problem sets in prompts
  • Connect to flexbook readings and lecture content
  • Use as formative assessment to guide instruction
  • Build toward unit summative assessments

Assessment and Feedback

  • Provide feedback on Forecast to guide learning
  • Use Blueprint to identify misconceptions before final learning
  • Use Reflection as significant summative assessment
  • Look for evidence of three-dimensional learning (SEPs, CCCs, DCIs)
