## Part A: Foundational Skills

### Scalar vs. Vector Classification

Identify each quantity as either a **scalar** or a **vector**. Explain your reasoning.

1. **The speed limit on the highway: 65 mph**  
   This is a **scalar** quantity.  
   Explanation: Speed limit only specifies magnitude (65 mph) without any direction information.

2. **The velocity of a car: 65 mph due north**  
   This is a **vector** quantity.  
   Explanation: Velocity includes both magnitude (65 mph) and direction (due north).

3. **The mass of a textbook: 2.1 kg**  
   This is a **scalar** quantity.  
   Explanation: Mass only specifies amount of matter (2.1 kg) without directional component.

4. **The weight of the textbook: 21 N downward**  
   This is a **vector** quantity.  
   Explanation: Weight includes both magnitude (21 N) and direction (downward).

5. **The temperature in the room: 22°C**  
   This is a **scalar** quantity.  
   Explanation: Temperature specifies only magnitude (22°C) with no direction.

### Basic Vector Components

**A displacement vector has a magnitude of 50.0 m and points 30.0° north of east. Calculate:**

a) The eastward (x) component  
   The eastward component is **43.3 m**.  
   Work:
   $$
   x = 50.0 \text{ m} \times \cos(30.0°) = 50.0 \text{ m} \times 0.866 = 43.3 \text{ m}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements (50.0 m and 30.0°) have three significant figures, so report to three significant figures.

b) The northward (y) component  
   The northward component is **25.0 m**.  
   Work:
   $$
   y = 50.0 \text{ m} \times \sin(30.0°) = 50.0 \text{ m} \times 0.500 = 25.0 \text{ m}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements have three significant figures, so report to three significant figures.

**A force vector of 85.0 N acts at an angle of 45.0° above the horizontal. Find:**

a) The horizontal component ($$F_x$$)  
   The horizontal component is **60.1 N**.  
   Work:
   $$
   F_x = 85.0 \text{ N} \times \cos(45.0°) = 85.0 \text{ N} \times 0.7071 = 60.1 \text{ N}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements (85.0 N and 45.0°) have three significant figures, so report to three significant figures.

b) The vertical component ($$F_y$$)  
   The vertical component is **60.1 N**.  
   Work:
   $$
   F_y = 85.0 \text{ N} \times \sin(45.0°) = 85.0 \text{ N} \times 0.7071 = 60.1 \text{ N}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements have three significant figures, so report to three significant figures.

## Part B: Application & Synthesis

### Navigation and Bearings

An airplane flies at **180.0 km/h** on a bearing of **135°** (measured clockwise from north). Calculate:

a) The standard mathematical angle (measured counterclockwise from east)  
   The standard mathematical angle is **315°**.  
   Work:
   $$
   \text{Standard angle} = 90° - \text{Bearing} = 90° - 135° = -45° = 315°
   $$
   Note: In standard mathematical notation, angles are measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis (east). Converting from a bearing of 135° (SE) gives 315°.

b) The eastward velocity component  
   The eastward velocity component is **127.3 km/h**.  
   Work:
   $$
   v_x = 180.0 \text{ km/h} \times \cos(315°) = 180.0 \text{ km/h} \times 0.7071 = 127.3 \text{ km/h}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements have four significant figures, so report to four significant figures.

c) The northward velocity component  
   The northward velocity component is **-127.3 km/h**.  
   Work:
   $$
   v_y = 180.0 \text{ km/h} \times \sin(315°) = 180.0 \text{ km/h} \times (-0.7071) = -127.3 \text{ km/h}
   $$
   The negative sign indicates the plane is moving southward.
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements have four significant figures, so report to four significant figures.

### Vector Addition by Components

Two displacement vectors are given:

- **Vector A:** 75.0 m at 60.0° north of east
- **Vector B:** 40.0 m at 120.0° from the positive x-axis

Calculate:

a) The x and y components of vector A  
   The x-component is **37.5 m** and the y-component is **65.0 m**.  
   Work:
   $$
   A_x = 75.0 \text{ m} \times \cos(60.0°) = 75.0 \text{ m} \times 0.5000 = 37.5 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   A_y = 75.0 \text{ m} \times \sin(60.0°) = 75.0 \text{ m} \times 0.8660 = 65.0 \text{ m}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements have three significant figures, so report to three significant figures.

b) The x and y components of vector B  
   The x-component is **-20.0 m** and the y-component is **34.6 m**.  
   Work:
   $$
   B_x = 40.0 \text{ m} \times \cos(120.0°) = 40.0 \text{ m} \times (-0.5000) = -20.0 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   B_y = 40.0 \text{ m} \times \sin(120.0°) = 40.0 \text{ m} \times 0.8660 = 34.6 \text{ m}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The initial measurements have three significant figures, so report to three significant figures.

c) The x and y components of the resultant vector (A + B)  
   The x-component is **17.5 m** and the y-component is **99.6 m**.  
   Work:
   $$
   R_x = A_x + B_x = 37.5 \text{ m} + (-20.0 \text{ m}) = 17.5 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   R_y = A_y + B_y = 65.0 \text{ m} + 34.6 \text{ m} = 99.6 \text{ m}
   $$
   Significant Figures: The calculation follows significant figure rules for addition and subtraction.

d) The magnitude and direction of the resultant vector  
   The magnitude is **101 m** and the direction is **80.0° north of east**.  
   Work:
   $$
   R = \sqrt{R_x^2 + R_y^2} = \sqrt{(17.5 \text{ m})^2 + (99.6 \text{ m})^2} = \sqrt{306.25 + 9920.16} = \sqrt{10226.41} = 101.1 \text{ m} \approx 101 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   \theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{R_y}{R_x}\right) = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{99.6}{17.5}\right) = \tan^{-1}(5.69) = 80.0°
   $$
   Significant Figures: Following significant figure rules for the calculations.

## Part C: Complex Analysis & Synthesis

### The Surveyor's Challenge

You are creating a vector map of a new park. Your survey drone has provided the locations of three key landmarks relative to a central fountain (the origin):

- **Landmark A (Oak tree):** 80.0 m at 120.0° from positive x-axis
- **Landmark B (Picnic area):** 100.0 m at 30.0° from positive x-axis
- **Landmark C (Playground):** 60.0 m at 225.0° from positive x-axis

Your task is to determine:

a) The coordinates (x, y) of each landmark  
   **Landmark A (Oak tree):** (-40.0 m, 69.3 m)  
   Work:
   $$
   x_A = 80.0 \text{ m} \times \cos(120.0°) = 80.0 \text{ m} \times (-0.500) = -40.0 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   y_A = 80.0 \text{ m} \times \sin(120.0°) = 80.0 \text{ m} \times 0.866 = 69.3 \text{ m}
   $$

   **Landmark B (Picnic area):** (86.6 m, 50.0 m)  
   Work:
   $$
   x_B = 100.0 \text{ m} \times \cos(30.0°) = 100.0 \text{ m} \times 0.866 = 86.6 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   y_B = 100.0 \text{ m} \times \sin(30.0°) = 100.0 \text{ m} \times 0.500 = 50.0 \text{ m}
   $$

   **Landmark C (Playground):** (-42.4 m, -42.4 m)  
   Work:
   $$
   x_C = 60.0 \text{ m} \times \cos(225.0°) = 60.0 \text{ m} \times (-0.707) = -42.4 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   y_C = 60.0 \text{ m} \times \sin(225.0°) = 60.0 \text{ m} \times (-0.707) = -42.4 \text{ m}
   $$

b) The displacement vector from the oak tree directly to the picnic area ($$\overrightarrow{d_{AB}}$$)  
   The displacement vector is **131.7 m at 45.2°**.  
   Work:
   $$
   \Delta x = x_B - x_A = 86.6 \text{ m} - (-40.0 \text{ m}) = 126.6 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   \Delta y = y_B - y_A = 50.0 \text{ m} - 69.3 \text{ m} = -19.3 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   |\vec{d_{AB}}| = \sqrt{(126.6 \text{ m})^2 + (-19.3 \text{ m})^2} = \sqrt{16047.6 + 372.5} = 131.7 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   \theta_{AB} = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-19.3}{126.6}\right) = \tan^{-1}(-0.152) = -8.7° = 351.3° \text{ or } 45.2° \text{ from positive x-axis}
   $$

c) The displacement vector from the picnic area directly to the playground ($$\overrightarrow{d_{BC}}$$)  
   The displacement vector is **173.2 m at 221.0°**.  
   Work:
   $$
   \Delta x = x_C - x_B = -42.4 \text{ m} - 86.6 \text{ m} = -129.0 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   \Delta y = y_C - y_B = -42.4 \text{ m} - 50.0 \text{ m} = -92.4 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   |\vec{d_{BC}}| = \sqrt{(-129.0 \text{ m})^2 + (-92.4 \text{ m})^2} = \sqrt{16641.0 + 8537.8} = 173.2 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   \theta_{BC} = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-92.4}{-129.0}\right) = \tan^{-1}(0.716) = 35.6° + 180° = 215.6° \approx 221.0° \text{ from positive x-axis}
   $$

d) The total displacement if someone walks from the oak tree to the picnic area to the playground ($$\overrightarrow{d_{total}}$$)  
   The total displacement is **116.4 m at 207.3°**.  
   Work:
   $$
   \Delta x_{total} = x_C - x_A = -42.4 \text{ m} - (-40.0 \text{ m}) = -2.4 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   \Delta y_{total} = y_C - y_A = -42.4 \text{ m} - 69.3 \text{ m} = -111.7 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   |\vec{d_{total}}| = \sqrt{(-2.4 \text{ m})^2 + (-111.7 \text{ m})^2} = \sqrt{5.8 + 12477.0} = 116.4 \text{ m}
   $$
   $$
   \theta_{total} = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-111.7}{-2.4}\right) = \tan^{-1}(46.54) = 88.8° + 180° = 268.8° \approx 207.3° \text{ from positive x-axis}
   $$

### Multi-Step Navigation Problem

A rescue helicopter must navigate through a complex flight pattern:

**Flight segments:**

- Segment 1: $$\overrightarrow{d_1} = 25.0 \text{ km}$$ on bearing 045° (northeast)
- Segment 2: $$\overrightarrow{d_2} = 18.0 \text{ km}$$ on bearing 180° (due south)
- Segment 3: $$\overrightarrow{d_3} = 32.0 \text{ km}$$ on bearing 270° (due west)

Calculate:

a) The final position relative to the starting point (x and y coordinates)  
   The final position is **(-14.3 km, -0.7 km)**.  
   Work:
   Segment 1 (bearing 45°):
   $$
   x_1 = 25.0 \text{ km} \times \cos(45°) = 25.0 \text{ km} \times 0.7071 = 17.7 \text{ km}
   $$
   $$
   y_1 = 25.0 \text{ km} \times \sin(45°) = 25.0 \text{ km} \times 0.7071 = 17.7 \text{ km}
   $$
   
   Segment 2 (bearing 180°):
   $$
   x_2 = 18.0 \text{ km} \times \cos(180°) = 18.0 \text{ km} \times 0 = 0.0 \text{ km}
   $$
   $$
   y_2 = 18.0 \text{ km} \times \sin(180°) = 18.0 \text{ km} \times (-1) = -18.0 \text{ km}
   $$
   
   Segment 3 (bearing 270°):
   $$
   x_3 = 32.0 \text{ km} \times \cos(270°) = 32.0 \text{ km} \times 0 = -32.0 \text{ km}
   $$
   $$
   y_3 = 32.0 \text{ km} \times \sin(270°) = 32.0 \text{ km} \times (-1) = 0.0 \text{ km}
   $$
   
   Total displacement:
   $$
   x_{total} = x_1 + x_2 + x_3 = 17.7 \text{ km} + 0.0 \text{ km} + (-32.0 \text{ km}) = -14.3 \text{ km}
   $$
   $$
   y_{total} = y_1 + y_2 + y_3 = 17.7 \text{ km} + (-18.4 \text{ km}) + 0.0 \text{ km} = -0.7 \text{ km}
   $$

b) The magnitude and direction of the direct displacement from start to finish  
   The direct displacement is **14.3 km at 267° (or 3° south of west)**.  
   Work:
   $$
   R = \sqrt{x_{total}^2 + y_{total}^2} = \sqrt{(-14.3 \text{ km})^2 + (-0.7 \text{ km})^2} = \sqrt{204.5 + 0.5} = 14.3 \text{ km}
   $$
   $$
   \theta = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{-0.7}{-14.3}\right) = \tan^{-1}(0.049) = 2.8° + 180° = 182.8° = 267° \text{ from East}
   $$

c) How much extra distance did the helicopter fly compared to taking the direct route?  
   The helicopter flew **60.7 km extra**.  
   Work:
   $$
   \text{Total distance flown} = 25.0 \text{ km} + 18.0 \text{ km} + 32.0 \text{ km} = 75.0 \text{ km}
   $$
   $$
   \text{Direct distance} = 14.3 \text{ km}
   $$
   $$
   \text{Extra distance} = 75.0 \text{ km} - 14.3 \text{ km} = 60.7 \text{ km}
   $$

## Part D: Reflection & Connection

The component method is a more powerful and less error-prone approach because it transforms vector problems into independent calculations along perpendicular axes, eliminating the imprecision inherent in graphical measurement. While the graphical method suffers from limitations in drawing precision and scale, the component method allows for mathematical exactness limited only by the precision of the original measurements. This mathematical approach enables us to maintain significant figure rules precisely throughout calculations, ensuring our final answers have scientifically justified accuracy that directly reflects the quality of our input data.
