### 2. Vector Components

a) Sketch this vector on a coordinate plane, labeling the vector $\vec{d}$ and its components, $d_x$ and $d_y$.

b) Calculate the eastward component $(d_x)$ of the displacement.

c) Calculate the northward component $(d_y)$ of the displacement.
