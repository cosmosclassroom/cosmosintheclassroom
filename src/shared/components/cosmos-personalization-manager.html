  • Rotation Day Display: 4-day rotation cycle (A, B, C, D)
  • Responsive Design: Mobile-friendly interface

System Architecture

The Chunker System: 4 chunks per class × 4 day rotations × 6 periods per day (dropping 2) = 144 total chunks per academic year

Quarter Structure: 36 chunks per quarter × 4 quarters = 144 total chunks

Unit Breakdown: Each unit has Beginning (4 chunks), Middle (8 chunks), End (4 chunks) = 16 chunks per unit

Course Integration: Honors Physics (Periods 1, 3, 7) and Standard Physics (Periods 2, 4, 6)
