# Answer Key

## Section 1: Dynamics
1. a = F/m = 15 N / 5.0 kg = 3.0 m/s² to the east

2. Forces along the slope:
   Fₓ = mg sin(20°) - μₖmg cos(20°)
   a = g[sin(20°) - μₖcos(20°)]
   a = 9.8[sin(20°) - 0.12cos(20°)]
   a = 9.8[0.342 - 0.12(0.940)]
   a = 9.8[0.342 - 0.113]
   a = 9.8(0.229) = 2.24 m/s²

3. Minimum force = μₛN = μₛmg = 0.30 × 2.0 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 5.88 N

4. a) First law (inertia)

5. F = μₛN = μₛmg = 0.40 × 50 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 196 N

## Section 2: Circular Motion and Gravitation
6. v = √(GM/r) where r = Earth radius + height = 6400 km + 3600 km = 10000 km = 1.0 × 10⁷ m
   v = √[(6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²)(6.0 × 10²⁴ kg)/(1.0 × 10⁷ m)]
   v = √[(4.0 × 10¹⁴)] = 6.3 × 10⁶ m/s

7. aₑ = v²/r = (15 m/s)²/50 m = 4.5 m/s²

8. b) Second law

9. T = m × aₑ = m × ω²r
   ω = 2πf = 2π × 2.0 rev/s = 4π rad/s
   T = 0.15 kg × (4π rad/s)² × 1.2 m
   T = 0.15 kg × 16π² rad²/s² × 1.2 m
   T = 0.15 kg × 16 × 9.87 × 1.2 m/s²
   T = 28.4 N

10. M = 4π²r³/(GT²)
    T = 27.3 days = 27.3 × 24 × 3600 s = 2.36 × 10⁶ s
    M = 4π²(3.84 × 10⁸ m)³/[(6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²)(2.36 × 10⁶ s)²]
    M = 4π²(5.66 × 10²⁵)/(6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 5.57 × 10¹²)
    M = 6.0 × 10²⁴ kg

## Section 3: Work and Energy
11. W = F × d = 8.0 N × 5.0 m = 40 J
    KE = W = 40 J

12. PE = mgh = 60 kg × 9.8 m/s² × 400 m = 235,200 J = 2.35 × 10⁵ J

13. PE = ½kx² = ½ × 200 N/m × (0.15 m)² = ½ × 200 × 0.0225 = 2.25 J

14. P = ΔKE/Δt = ½mv²/t = ½ × 1500 kg × (25 m/s)²/10 s = 46,875 W = 4.7 × 10⁴ W

15. a) When force and displacement are in opposite directions

## Section 4: Harmonics
16. T = 2π√(L/g)
    L = g(T/2π)² = 9.8 m/s² × (2.0 s/2π)² = 9.8 × (0.318)² = 9.8 × 0.101 = 0.99 m

17. E = ½kA² = 6.0 J
    For a simple harmonic oscillator, k = m(2πf)²
    k = 0.50 kg × (2π × 2.0 Hz)² = 0.50 kg × (4π)² Hz² = 0.50 × 16π² = 79 N/m
    A² = 2E/k = 2 × 6.0 J / 79 N/m = 0.152 m²
    A = 0.39 m

18. b) Destructive interference

19. λ = v/f = 340 m/s / 680 Hz = 0.50 m

20. a) At the equilibrium position

## Section 5: Electromagnetism
21. F = k|q₁q₂|/r² = (9.0 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²) × |5.0 × 10⁻⁶ C × (-3.0 × 10⁻⁶ C)|/(0.40 m)²
    F = 9.0 × 10⁹ × 15 × 10⁻¹² / 0.16
    F = 9.0 × 15 × 10⁻³ / 0.16 = 0.84 N

22. F = qE = (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) × (4.0 × 10³ N/C) = 6.4 × 10⁻¹⁶ N

23. d) Nuclear decay

24. V ∝ 1/r, so V(0.40 m) = V(0.20 m) × (0.20 m/0.40 m) = 600 V × 0.5 = 300 V

25. C = κε₀A/d where κ is the dielectric constant
    κ = Cd/(ε₀A) = (1.77 × 10⁻¹⁰ F)(2.0 × 10⁻³ m)/[(8.85 × 10⁻¹² F/m)(4.0 × 10⁻² m²)]
    κ = 3.54 × 10⁻¹³ / 3.54 × 10⁻¹³ = 1.0 (vacuum or air)

## Section 6: Electricity
26. V = IR = 3.0 A × 5.0 Ω = 15 V

27. R = V/I = 120 V / 8.0 A = 15 Ω

28. Rₑq = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ = 3.0 Ω + 6.0 Ω + 9.0 Ω = 18 Ω

29. 1/Rₑq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ = 1/3.0 + 1/6.0 + 1/9.0 = 0.333 + 0.167 + 0.111 = 0.611
    Rₑq = 1/0.611 = 1.64 Ω

30. Vₜ = ε - Ir = 12 V - (I × 0.50 Ω)
    I = ε/(R + r) = 12 V / (5.5 Ω + 0.50 Ω) = 12 V / 6.0 Ω = 2.0 A
    Vₜ = 12 V - (2.0 A × 0.50 Ω) = 12 V - 1.0 V = 11 V
