Teaching Strategies to Maximize Time:

Flipped Classroom Components: Assign reading or video tutorials before introducing new topics
Focused Problem Sets: Limit to 5-7 high-quality problems that target essential skills
In-Class Practice: Use 15-20 minutes of class time for guided practice
Concept Connections: Regularly highlight connections between topics
Visual Demonstrations: Use simulations or quick demos to reinforce concepts
Formative Assessment: Use entrance/exit tickets to quickly check understanding

Time-Saving Tips:

Create a one-page formula sheet for students to use
Focus lab activities on conceptual understanding rather than extensive data collection
Use online simulations (like PhET) to demonstrate concepts quickly
Prepare condensed handouts with key definitions and examples
Consider a final project that integrates multiple concepts rather than separate assessments for each unit
