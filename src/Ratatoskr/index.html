Welcome to Ratatoskr

This floating widget represents your academic progress tracker, inspired by the Norse squirrel who carries messages between the roots and crown of Yggdrasil.

Click the squirrel icon in the top right to see your daily chunk progression. The widget shows your current position in the academic day, with completed tasks in green, your current focus in orange, and upcoming chunks in purple.

The progress bar fills throughout the day, and the shimmer effect represents Ratatoskr scurrying along the tree of time, carrying your academic progress from root to branch.

This prototype demonstrates the core concept - a non-intrusive floating widget that expands to show detailed progress when needed. The real version would integrate with your WeekCentricChunkerEngine to display actual curriculum data.
