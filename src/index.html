About

Cosmos in the Classroom provides a comprehensive physics curriculum that emphasizes conceptual understanding through real-world phenomena. Our interactive portal system allows students to track their progress through carefully designed units that build from fundamental measurement concepts to advanced topics in waves, optics, and electricity.
