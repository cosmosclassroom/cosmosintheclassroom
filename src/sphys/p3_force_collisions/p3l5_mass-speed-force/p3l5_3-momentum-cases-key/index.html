Warm-up Problems

Warm-up 1 Solutions

Given:

  • Bowling ball: $m_A = 5$ kg, $v_A = 8$ m/s, $v’_A = 6$ m/s
  • Pin: $m_B = 2$ kg, $v_B = 0$ m/s, $v’_B = ?$

a) Initial momentum of bowling ball: \(p_{A,i} = m_A \cdot v_A = 5 \text{ kg} \times 8 \text{ m/s} = 40 \text{ kg⋅m/s}\)

b) Final momentum of bowling ball: \(p'_{A} = m_A \cdot v'_A = 5 \text{ kg} \times 6 \text{ m/s} = 30 \text{ kg⋅m/s}\)

c) Final velocity of pin: Using conservation of momentum (two-object system): \(m_A v_A + m_B v_B = m_A v'_A + m_B v'_B\) \(40 + 0 = 30 + 2v'_B\) \(10 = 2v'_B\) \(v'_B = \frac{}{}$\)v’_B = 5 \text{ m/s}$$

d) Collision type: This is an inelastic collision because the objects separate after impact but do not stick together. In this course, we classify collisions as:

  • Elastic: Objects bounce off each other and separate completely
  • Inelastic: Objects stick together after collision (perfectly inelastic) or separate but with reduced relative motion

Since the bowling ball and pin separate after collision, this is inelastic.

Warm-up 2 Solutions

Given:

  • Car: $m_A = 1200$ kg, $v_A = 20$ m/s
  • Truck: $m_B = 1500$ kg, $v_B = 0$ m/s
  • After collision: both move together at $v’$

a) Total momentum before collision: \(p_{\text{total}} = m_A v_A + m_B v_B = 1200 \times 20 + 1500 \times 0 = 24,000 \text{ kg⋅m/s}\)

b) Final velocity: Using conservation of momentum: \(m_A v_A + m_B v_B = (m_A + m_B)v'\) \(24,000 = (1200 + 1500)v'\) \(24,000 = 2700v'\) \(v' = 8.89 \text{ m/s}\)

c) Collision type: This is an inelastic collision (specifically, perfectly inelastic) because the objects stick together after impact.

Question 1 Solutions

Given:

  • Cart A: $m_A = 60$ kg, $v_A = 0.50$ m/s, $v’_A = 0.25$ m/s
  • Cart B: $m_B = ?$, $v_B = 0$ m/s, $v’_B = 0.75$ m/s

1a) Collision type: This is an inelastic collision. The objects separate after collision but do not stick together. Since they don’t stick together completely, this is classified as inelastic rather than perfectly inelastic.

1b) Mass of cart B: Using conservation of momentum: \(m_A v_A + m_B v_B = m_A v'_A + m_B v'_B\) \(60 \times 0.50 + m_B \times 0 = 60 \times 0.25 + m_B \times 0.75\) \(30 = 15 + 0.75m_B\) \(15 = 0.75m_B\) \(m_B = 20 \text{ kg}\)

1c) Velocity change comparison:

  • Cart A: $\Delta v_A = v’_A - v_A = 0.25 - 0.50 = -0.25$ m/s
  • Cart B: $\Delta v_B = v’_B - v_B = 0.75 - 0 = 0.75$ m/s
Magnitude comparison: $\frac{ \Delta v_B }{ \Delta v_A } = \frac{0.75}{0.25} = 3$

Cart B’s velocity change is 3 times larger than cart A’s.

Question 2 Solutions

Given:

  • Small car: $m_s = 1500$ kg, $v_s = +15$ m/s
  • Large car: $m_L = 2250$ kg, $v_L = -20$ m/s, $v’_L = 0$ m/s
  • Find: $v’_s$

Solution: Using conservation of momentum: \(m_s v_s + m_L v_L = m_s v'_s + m_L v'_L\) \(1500 \times 15 + 2250 \times (-20) = 1500 \times v'_s + 2250 \times 0\) \(22,500 - 45,000 = 1500v'_s\) \(-22,500 = 1500v'_s\) \(v'_s = -15 \text{ m/s}\)

The smaller car should have a final velocity of -15 m/s (opposite to its initial direction).

Question 3 Solutions

Given:

  • Moving tanker: mass = $m$, initial velocity = $v_i$
  • Stationary cars: total mass = $11m$, initial velocity = 0
  • After collision: all 12 cars move together at $v_f = 0.2$ m/s
  • Individual tanker mass: $m = 119,000$ kg

3a) Collision type: This is an inelastic collision (specifically, perfectly inelastic) because the cars couple together and move as one unit after collision.

3b) Initial velocity calculation: Using conservation of momentum: \(m \cdot v_i + 11m \cdot 0 = (m + 11m) \cdot v_f\) \(m \cdot v_i = 12m \cdot 0.2\) \(v_i = 12 \times 0.2 = 2.4 \text{ m/s}\)

The tanker car must have been moving at 2.4 m/s before the collision.

Verification:

  • Initial momentum: $p_i = 119,000 \times 2.4 = 285,600$ kg⋅m/s
  • Final momentum: $p’_f = 12 \times 119,000 \times 0.2 = 285,600$ kg⋅m/s ✓
