# ELI5: Superposition and Wave Interference

**Version 1: Focus on Color Mixing**

**Student Prompt:** Imagine you're explaining to a five-year-old why soap bubbles show all the colors of the rainbow. They know colors mix to make new colors, but they don’t know about waves or anything like that. Explain to them *why* a soap bubble isn't just one color. Use simple words!

**Sample Student Response:** "Imagine you have red paint, blue paint, and yellow paint. When you mix them, you get new colors, right? A soap bubble is kind of like mixing colors, but instead of paint, it's using light! The bubble has tiny layers, and when light shines on it, some colors bounce off the layers a little bit more than others. That’s why we see all the colors – it’s like the bubble is showing us all the colors of light at once!"

---

**Version 2: Focus on Bouncing Light**

**Student Prompt:** Pretend you're talking to a five-year-old who knows that light bounces off things. Explain why soap bubbles show all the colors of the rainbow. Don't use big words like "superposition" or "interference." Just explain how the light is acting.

**Sample Student Response:** "When light shines on a soap bubble, it bounces off the top and the bottom. But some colors bounce off a little bit *more* than others. It's like some colors are better at bouncing than others! That's why we see all the colors – because they're all bouncing off in different ways."

---

**Version 3: Focus on Thin Layers**

**Student Prompt:** Imagine you're explaining to a five-year-old why soap bubbles show all the colors of the rainbow. They know that some things are thin, like paper, and some are thick. Explain why the bubble shows all the colors.

**Sample Student Response:** "A soap bubble is super, super thin, like a tiny piece of paper! When light goes into the bubble, some of it bounces back. Different colors bounce back a little differently because the bubble is just the right thickness for each color. That's why we see all the colors – because each color is bouncing back in its own special way!"
