Introduction to Wave Polarity

Electromagnetic waves consist of oscillating electric and magnetic fields that are perpendicular to each other and to the direction of wave propagation. Polarity refers to the orientation of these oscillations.

Key Point: Understanding wave polarity is crucial for technologies like antennas, polarized sunglasses, and fiber optic communications.

Polarization States

  • Linear Polarization: The electric field oscillates in a single plane
  • Circular Polarization: The electric field rotates as the wave propagates
  • Elliptical Polarization: A combination of linear and circular polarization
