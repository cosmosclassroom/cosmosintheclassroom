# Wave Equation Practice Problems
## P5L9 Student Solution Key

### Solutions:

1. A wave has a frequency of $2.5 \times 10^3$ Hz and a wavelength of $0.4$ meters. What is the speed of the wave?

    | | $v = f \cdot \lambda$ |
    |---:|:---|
    | $f = 2.5 \times 10^3 \, Hz$ <br> $\lambda = 0.4 \, m$ | $v = (2.5 \times 10^3 \, Hz)(0.4 \, m)$ <br> $v = 1.0 \times 10^3\, m/s$|

2.  A sound wave travels at a speed of 343 m/s and has a frequency of 500 Hz. What is its wavelength?

    | | $v = f \cdot \lambda$ |
    |---|:---|
    | $f = 500 \, Hz$ <br> $v = 343 \, m/s$ | $\lambda = \frac{v}{f}$ <br> $\lambda = \frac{343\, m/s}{500\, Hz}$ <br> $\lambda = 6.86 \times 10^-1 \,m \text{ or } 0.686 \,m$|

3.  A radio wave has a wavelength of 300 meters and travels at the speed of light. What is the frequency of the radio wave?

    ||$v = f \cdot \lambda$|
    |-:|:-|
    |$v = 3.0 \times 10^8 \, m/s$ <br> $\lambda = 300 \,m$|$f = \frac{v}{\lambda}$ <br> $f = \frac{3.0 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{300 \text{ m}}$ <br> $f = 1.0 \times 10^6 \text{ Hz}$|

4.  A wave on a string has a speed of 50 m/s and a wavelength of 2.0 meters. What is the frequency of the wave?

    ||$v = f \cdot \lambda$|
    |-:|:-|
    |$v = 50 \, m/s$ <br> $\lambda = 2.0 \, m$|$f = \frac{v}{\lambda}$ <br> $f = \frac{50 \text{ m/s}}{2.0 \text{ m}}$ <br> $f = 25 \text{ Hz} = 2.5 \times 10^1 \text{ Hz}$|

5.  An electromagnetic wave has a frequency of 100 MHz. What is its wavelength in a vacuum?

    First, convert MHz to Hz:

    $100 \, MHz \times \frac{1.0 \times 10^6 \, Hz}{ 1 \,MHz} = 1.00 \times 10^8 \,Hz$

    ||$v = f \cdot \lambda$|
    |-:|:-|
    |$v = 3.0 \times 10^8 \, m/s$ <br> $f = 1.00 \times 10^8 \, Hz$|$ \lambda = \frac{v}{f}$ <br> $\lambda = \frac{3.0 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{1.0 \times 10^8 \text{ Hz}}$ <br> $\lambda = 3.0 \text{ m}$|

6. A wave has a wavelength of $5.0 \times 10^{-7}$ meters and a speed of $3.0 \times 10^8$ m/s. What is its frequency?

    ||$v = f \cdot \lambda$|
    |--:|:-|
    |$v = 3.0 \times 10^8 \, m/s$ <br> $\lambda = 5.0 \times 10^{-7} \, m$|$f = \frac{v}{\lambda}$ <br> $f = \frac{3.0 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}}{5.0 \times 10^{-7} \text{ m}}$ <br> $f = 6.0 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$|


7. A sound wave in water has a frequency of 1 kHz and a wavelength of 1.43 meters. What is the speed of sound in water?
    First, convert KHz to Hz:

   $1.0 KHz \times \frac{1 \times 10^3 \, Hz}{1 KHz} = 1.0 \times 10^3 \, Hz$

   | | $v = f \cdot \lambda$ |
   |---:|:---|
   |$f = 1.0 \times 10^3 \, Hz$ <br> $\lambda =1.43 \,m$ | $v =(1.0 \times 10^3 \, Hz)(1.43m)$ <br> $v=1.43 \times 10^ 3 \,m/s$ |

8. A seismic wave travels at a speed of $7.2 \times 10^3$ m/s and has a frequency of 20 Hz. What is its wavelength?
   
    |  | $v = f \cdot \lambda$ |
    |---|---|
    | $v = 7.2 \times 10^3 \, m/s$ <br> $f = 20 \,Hz$| $\lambda = \frac{v}{f}$ <br> $\lambda = \frac{7.2 \times 10^3 \,m/s}{20 \, Hz}$ <br> $\lambda = 3.6 \times 10 ^2\, m$|
    

9.  A light wave has a wavelength of 450 nm. What is its frequency in a vacuum? (speed of light = $3.0 \times 10^8$ m/s)

    First, convert nm to m:

    $$450 \, nm \times \frac{1\times 10^{-9}\,m}{1 nm} = 4.5 \times 10^{-7} m$$

    |  | $c = f \cdot \lambda$|
    |---|---|
    | $c = 3.0 \times 10^8 \, m/s$ <br> $\lambda = 4.5 \times 10^{-7} \, m$|$f = \frac{c}{\lambda}$ <br> $f = \frac{3.0 \times 10^8 \, m/s}{4.5 \times 10^-7 \, Hz}$ <br>  $f ≈6.67×10^{14} Hz$|

10. A wave has a speed of $2.0 \times 10^7$ m/s and a frequency of 4.0 MHz. What is its wavelength?
    
    First, convert MHz to Hz.:
    $4.0 MHz \times \frac{1.0 \times 10^6 \, Hz}{1 MHz} = 4.0 \times 10^6 Hz$

    ||$v = f \cdot \lambda$|
    |-:|:-|
    |$v = 2.0 \times 10^7 \, m/s$ <br> $ f = 4.0 \times 10^6 \, Hz$ | $\lambda = \frac{v}{f}$ <br> $\lambda = \frac{2.0 \times 10^7 \text{ m/s}}{4.0 \times 10^6 \text{ Hz}}$ <br> $\lambda = 5.0 \, m$|
