**Hexagon Laboratories**
**Article Review: 2021 Texas Blackouts**


**Objective:** To practice obtaining, evaluating, and communicating information from multiple, sometimes conflicting, sources.

##### **Shared Articles: Building Context (Articles 1-3)**

| Interpretation Questions |
| :--- |
| **Obtaining Information:** Based on the first three articles, create a timeline of the crisis. What were the key events and when did they happen? |
| |
| **Evaluating Information:** How does the personal story of Stephanie Murdoch in Article 3 help you understand the data presented in Article 1 (the death toll)?  |
| |
| **Communicating Information:** Imagine you are explaining this crisis to a friend. What are the three most critical pieces of information from these articles that you would share to convey the severity of the situation? |

##### **Conflicting Reports: The Blame Game (Articles 4 & 5)**

| Interpretation Questions for the Group Reading Article 4 ("Windmills Are Blamed") |

| Interpretation Questions for the Group Reading Article 5 ("Wind Turbines Don't Deserve the Blame") |

##### **The Human Cost: A Deeper Look (Article 6)**

| Interpretation Questions |
| :--- |
