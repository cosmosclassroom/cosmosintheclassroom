Standard Physics Assessment: Analyzing the Texas Power Crisis

Anchoring Phenomenon: The Texas power crisis of February 2021, where a severe winter storm caused a massive failure of the state’s electrical grid.

Core Concepts: Electrical Circuits, Energy Transfer, Systems & System Models, Engineering Trade-offs

Time Limit: 50 minutes

Support Materials Available: Formula Sheet, Calculator

Assessment Overview What You’ll Demonstrate By completing this assessment, you’ll show your ability to:

Analyze simple circuits using Ohm’s Law.

Explain the causes of a large-scale system failure using data.

Evaluate competing engineering solutions based on criteria and constraints.

How This Assessment Works Real-World Focus: All questions connect to the Texas power crisis.

Scaffolded Support: Each section builds from foundational knowledge to complex application.

Multiple Formats: You will answer multiple-choice, short answer, and data analysis questions.
