Part C: Circuit Models and Texas Crisis

7a) Both use parallel circuits so if one part fails, other parts keep working. In houses, if one light breaks, others stay on. In the grid, if one power plant fails, others can still provide power to different areas. 7b) When the power strip switch was OFF, all outlets lost power. Similarly, when grid operators shut down parts of the system, entire regions lost power to prevent larger system damage. 8) Various answers from personal stories: missed school celebrations, family stress, health impacts, etc.

Part D: Systems Thinking

8) Sample answer: When natural gas wells froze, those power plants couldn’t generate electricity. This meant less total power available. The remaining plants couldn’t meet demand, so operators had to shut off power to different areas to prevent the whole system from failing completely.
