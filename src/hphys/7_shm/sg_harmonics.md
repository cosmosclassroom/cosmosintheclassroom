# Waves and Harmonics Study Guide

## What You Should Know

### Key Concepts
- [ ] Define and explain Simple Harmonic Motion (SHM)
- [ ] Describe damped motion and how it differs from undamped motion
- [ ] Differentiate between mechanical and electromagnetic waves
- [ ] Compare and contrast transverse and longitudinal waves
- [ ] Explain how energy is transmitted through waves
- [ ] Define wavelength, amplitude, period, and frequency
- [ ] Identify the formula for calculating the period of a simple pendulum or spring-mass system
- [ ] Understand the relationship between frequency and period
- [ ] Recognize the wave speed equation and explain each variable
- [ ] Explain Hooke's Law and how it relates to simple harmonic motion


## What You Should Be Able to Do

- [ ] Calculate the period of a pendulum given its length
- [ ] Convert between frequency and period values
- [ ] Determine wave speed using wavelength and frequency data
- [ ] Apply Hooke's Law to spring systems
- [ ] Analyze the motion of objects undergoing SHM
- [ ] Predict how changing one variable affects other variables in wave equations
- [ ] Predict how waves would interact with each other during superposition
- [ ] Compare energy transfer in waves of different amplitudes
