# 8-Week Physics Curriculum for Honors Students

## Week 1: Simple Harmonic Motion (Essentials)
### Session 1 (85 min): Basic SHM Concepts and Hooke's Law
- **Scope:**
  - Definition and examples of simple harmonic motion
  - Hooke's Law: $F = -kx$
  - Equilibrium and restoring forces
  - Spring constant and its physical meaning
  - Demonstration with springs and pendulums
- **Sequence:**
  1. Introduction to oscillatory motion (15 min)
  2. Mathematical definition of SHM (20 min)
  3. Hooke's Law derivation and application (25 min)
  4. In-class demonstrations (15 min)
  5. Guided practice problems (10 min)

### Session 2 (85 min): Period and Frequency
- **Scope:**
  - Period and frequency relationship: $f = 1/T$
  - Period formula for mass-spring system: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$
  - Period formula for pendulum: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g}$
  - Energy in SHM systems (qualitative)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of previous session (10 min)
  2. Period and frequency definitions (15 min)
  3. Derivation of period formulas (25 min)
  4. Energy transformations in SHM (20 min)
  5. Worked examples (15 min)

### Session 3 (45 min): Problem-solving with SHM Equations
- **Scope:**
  - Application of SHM equations to real-world problems
  - Relationship between displacement, velocity, and acceleration
- **Sequence:**
  1. Quick review of key equations (5 min)
  2. Guided problem-solving session (30 min)
  3. Discussion of common misconceptions (10 min)

## Week 2: Basic Waves
### Session 1 (85 min): Wave Properties
- **Scope:**
  - Types of waves: transverse and longitudinal
  - Wave characteristics: amplitude, wavelength, frequency
  - Wave speed equation: $v = f\lambda$
  - Wave energy and intensity (qualitative)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Introduction to waves and classification (20 min)
  2. Mathematical description of waves (25 min)
  3. Wave speed derivation (15 min)
  4. Wave demonstrations (15 min)
  5. Practice with wave equations (10 min)

### Session 2 (85 min): Wave Behavior
- **Scope:**
  - Reflection and transmission
  - Interference: constructive and destructive
  - Superposition principle
  - Standing waves (qualitative)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of wave properties (10 min)
  2. Reflection and transmission principles (20 min)
  3. Interference patterns and superposition (25 min)
  4. Introduction to standing waves (20 min)
  5. Problem-solving with wave interference (10 min)

### Session 3 (45 min): Wave Practice Problems
- **Scope:**
  - Comprehensive application of wave concepts
  - Wave speed, frequency, and wavelength calculations
- **Sequence:**
  1. Brief review of key wave equations (5 min)
  2. Guided practice problems (30 min)
  3. Connecting waves to upcoming sound topic (10 min)

## Week 3: Sound
### Session 1 (85 min): Sound Wave Basics
- **Scope:**
  - Sound as pressure waves
  - Speed of sound: $v_{sound} ≈ 343 \text{ m/s}$ in air at room temperature
  - Factors affecting sound speed
  - Pitch, loudness, and quality
- **Sequence:**
  1. Nature of sound waves (20 min)
  2. Speed of sound in various media (15 min)
  3. Characteristics of sound (20 min)
  4. Sound demonstrations (20 min)
  5. Sound wave calculations (10 min)

### Session 2 (85 min): Resonance
- **Scope:**
  - Resonance in strings: $f_n = \frac{n \cdot v}{2L}$
  - Open pipes: $f_n = \frac{n \cdot v}{2L}$
  - Closed pipes: $f_n = \frac{n \cdot v}{4L}$ (odd harmonics only)
  - Musical instruments as examples
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of standing waves (15 min)
  2. String resonance (20 min)
  3. Air column resonance (20 min)
  4. Applications to musical instruments (20 min)
  5. Resonance calculations (10 min)

### Session 3 (45 min): Sound Intensity
- **Scope:**
  - Sound intensity: $I = \frac{P}{A}$
  - Decibel scale: $\beta = 10 \log(\frac{I}{I_0})$, where $I_0 = 10^{-12} \text{ W/m}^2$
  - Hearing range and thresholds
- **Sequence:**
  1. Introduction to sound intensity (10 min)
  2. Logarithmic scales and decibels (15 min)
  3. Practice with decibel calculations (15 min)
  4. Sound applications wrap-up (5 min)

## Week 4: Electrostatics Basics
### Session 1 (85 min): Electric Charge and Coulomb's Law
- **Scope:**
  - Electric charge properties
  - Conservation of charge
  - Coulomb's law: $F = k\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2}$
  - Coulomb constant: $k = 9 \times 10^9 \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$
- **Sequence:**
  1. Introduction to electric charge (15 min)
  2. Charging methods demonstration (15 min)
  3. Coulomb's law derivation (20 min)
  4. Force calculations between point charges (25 min)
  5. Practice problems (10 min)

### Session 2 (85 min): Electric Field Concept
- **Scope:**
  - Electric field definition: $E = \frac{F}{q}$
  - Field due to point charge: $E = k\frac{q}{r^2}$
  - Field lines representation
  - Superposition of electric fields (algebraic only)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of Coulomb's law (10 min)
  2. Introduction to field concept (20 min)
  3. Electric field calculations (25 min)
  4. Field line demonstrations (15 min)
  5. Superposition principles (15 min)

### Session 3 (45 min): Practice with Coulomb's Law
- **Scope:**
  - Multi-charge systems
  - Force and field calculations
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of key electrostatic equations (5 min)
  2. Guided problem-solving session (35 min)
  3. Connection to upcoming potential concepts (5 min)

## Week 5: Electric Potential
### Session 1 (85 min): Electric Potential Energy and Potential
- **Scope:**
  - Electric potential energy: $U = k\frac{q_1q_2}{r}$
  - Electric potential: $V = k\frac{q}{r}$
  - Relationship between field and potential: $E = -\frac{\Delta V}{\Delta r}$ (in one dimension)
  - Equipotential surfaces (qualitative)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of electric field concept (10 min)
  2. Electric potential energy (25 min)
  3. Electric potential concept (25 min)
  4. Field-potential relationship (15 min)
  5. Practice calculations (10 min)

### Session 2 (85 min): Simple Capacitors
- **Scope:**
  - Capacitance definition: $C = \frac{Q}{V}$
  - Parallel plate capacitor: $C = \frac{\varepsilon_0 A}{d}$
  - Energy stored: $U = \frac{1}{2}QV = \frac{1}{2}CV^2$
  - Dielectrics (qualitative only)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of electric potential (10 min)
  2. Introduction to capacitors (20 min)
  3. Parallel plate capacitor derivation (20 min)
  4. Energy storage in capacitors (15 min)
  5. Dielectric effects (10 min)
  6. Capacitor calculations (10 min)

### Session 3 (45 min): Capacitor Applications
- **Scope:**
  - Problem-solving with capacitors
  - Real-world applications
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of capacitor equations (5 min)
  2. Guided practice with capacitor problems (30 min)
  3. Discussion of applications in technology (10 min)

## Week 6: Basic Circuits - Part 1
### Session 1 (85 min): Current, Resistance, and Ohm's Law
- **Scope:**
  - Electric current: $I = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}$
  - Resistance: $R = \frac{V}{I}$
  - Ohm's law: $V = IR$
  - Conductors and insulators
- **Sequence:**
  1. Introduction to electric current (20 min)
  2. Resistance concept and Ohm's law (25 min)
  3. Simple circuit demonstrations (15 min)
  4. Ohm's law problem solving (15 min)
  5. Preview of circuit analysis (10 min)

### Session 2 (85 min): Resistivity and Temperature Effects
- **Scope:**
  - Resistivity: $R = \rho\frac{L}{A}$
  - Temperature coefficient of resistance: $R = R_0[1 + \alpha(T - T_0)]$
  - Conductivity vs. resistivity
  - Material properties
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of resistance (10 min)
  2. Resistivity concept (20 min)
  3. Geometric factors affecting resistance (20 min)
  4. Temperature effects (20 min)
  5. Practice with resistivity problems (15 min)

### Session 3 (45 min): Analyzing Simple Circuits
- **Scope:**
  - Simple circuit diagrams
  - Single resistor circuits
  - Battery and internal resistance (qualitative)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Circuit diagram symbols and conventions (10 min)
  2. Single-loop circuit analysis (15 min)
  3. Problem-solving practice (20 min)

## Week 7: Basic Circuits - Part 2
### Session 1 (85 min): Series and Parallel Resistors
- **Scope:**
  - Series resistors: $R_{eq} = R_1 + R_2 + R_3 + ...$
  - Parallel resistors: $\frac{1}{R_{eq}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3} + ...$
  - Current and voltage in series and parallel
  - Combination circuits (simple cases only)
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of simple circuits (10 min)
  2. Series circuits (20 min)
  3. Parallel circuits (20 min)
  4. Series-parallel combinations (20 min)
  5. Practice circuit analysis (15 min)

### Session 2 (85 min): Electrical Power and Energy
- **Scope:**
  - Power: $P = VI = I^2R = \frac{V^2}{R}$
  - Energy: $E = Pt$
  - Kilowatt-hours
  - Electrical safety basics
- **Sequence:**
  1. Introduction to electrical power (20 min)
  2. Power in resistive circuits (20 min)
  3. Energy consumption calculations (15 min)
  4. Household electricity basics (15 min)
  5. Problem-solving with power equations (15 min)

### Session 3 (45 min): Circuit Problem-solving
- **Scope:**
  - Comprehensive circuit analysis
  - Power and energy calculations
- **Sequence:**
  1. Review of circuit analysis techniques (5 min)
  2. Guided problem-solving session (35 min)
  3. Preview of final review session (5 min)
