Group 3: Quantum Optics and Modern Physics
This group addresses the particle nature of light and related concepts:

Photon energy (E = hf = hc/λ)
Einstein's equation (E = mc²)
Planck's constant (h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s)
Wave-particle duality (combining E = hf with c = λf)

Learning focus: Understanding the quantum nature of light, photon energy, and connections to modern physics.
