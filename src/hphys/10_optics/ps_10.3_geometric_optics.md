Group 2: Geometric Optics and Ray Tracing
This group covers reflection, refraction, and lens behavior:

Law of reflection (θᵢ = θᵣ)
Snell's law (n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂)
Thin lens equation (1/s + 1/s' = 1/f and 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ)
Magnification formula (M = -dᵢ/dₒ)
Angular magnification (M = θ'/θ)
Total internal reflection condition (sin θᵢ > n₂/n₁)

Learning focus: Understanding image formation, reflection and refraction principles, and optical instrument basics.


# Geometric Optics Problem Set

## Multiple Choice and Short Answer Questions

**1. Basic Reflection (Easy)**  
A light ray strikes a mirror at an angle of 35° to the normal. What is the angle of reflection?

**2. Understanding Refraction (Easy)**  
When light passes from air into water, it:
   a) Speeds up and bends away from the normal
   b) Speeds up and bends toward the normal
   c) Slows down and bends away from the normal
   d) Slows down and bends toward the normal

**3. Index of Refraction (Medium)**  
The speed of light in a certain type of glass is 2.00 × 10⁸ m/s. Calculate the index of refraction of this glass. (Speed of light in vacuum = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s)

**4. Thin Lens Equation (Medium)**  
An object is placed 30 cm in front of a converging lens with a focal length of 15 cm. Calculate:
   a) The image distance
   b) Is the image real or virtual?

**5. Total Internal Reflection (Medium)**  
Light travels from water (n = 1.33) into air (n = 1.00). Calculate the critical angle above which total internal reflection occurs.

## Word Problems

**6. Magnification Problem (Medium)**  
A 2.5 cm tall object is placed 20 cm from a convex lens with a focal length of 10 cm.
   a) Calculate the location of the image.
   b) Calculate the height of the image.
   c) Is the image upright or inverted?

**7. Compound Lens System (Hard)**  
Two converging lenses with focal lengths f₁ = 15 cm and f₂ = 20 cm are placed 25 cm apart. An object is positioned 25 cm in front of the first lens.
   a) Find the final image position relative to the second lens.
   b) Calculate the overall magnification of the system.

**8. Optical Instrument Application (Hard)**  
A microscope has an objective lens with a focal length of 5.0 mm and an eyepiece with a focal length of 2.5 cm. The lenses are separated by 20 cm.
   a) Calculate the angular magnification when viewing a specimen placed 5.5 mm from the objective lens.
   b) If the final image needs to be at infinity, what should be the distance between the specimen and the objective lens?

**9. Real-World Application (Medium)**  
A diver underwater (n = 1.33) looks up at the water surface. The diver sees a circular "window" to the air above, beyond which everything appears dark.
   a) Explain this phenomenon using the principles of total internal reflection.
   b) Calculate the angular diameter of this circular window as seen by the diver.

**10. Ray Tracing Challenge (Hard)**  
A 3.0 cm tall object is placed 15 cm to the left of a concave mirror with a focal length of 10 cm. A converging lens with focal length 20 cm is placed 40 cm to the right of the mirror.
   a) Find the location and size of the final image.
   b) Draw a ray diagram showing how the light travels through this system.
   c) Is the final image real or virtual? Upright or inverted?

## Answer Key (For Teacher Use)

1. 35° (Law of reflection: θᵢ = θᵣ)

2. d) Slows down and bends toward the normal

3. n = c/v = 3.00 × 10⁸ / 2.00 × 10⁸ = 1.50

4. a) Using 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ → 1/15 = 1/30 + 1/dᵢ → dᵢ = 30 cm
   b) Real (positive image distance on opposite side of lens)

5. sin θc = n₂/n₁ = 1.00/1.33 = 0.752, so θc = 48.8°

6. a) 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ → 1/10 = 1/20 + 1/dᵢ → dᵢ = 20 cm
   b) M = -dᵢ/dₒ = -20/20 = -1, so height = -1 × 2.5 cm = -2.5 cm
   c) Inverted (negative magnification)

7. a) First image: 1/15 = 1/25 + 1/dᵢ₁ → dᵢ₁ = 37.5 cm
      This image is 37.5 - 25 = 12.5 cm from second lens
      Final image: 1/20 = 1/12.5 + 1/dᵢ₂ → dᵢ₂ = -30 cm
   b) M = (-37.5/25)×(-30/12.5) = 3.6

8. a) For objective: dᵢ₁ = (f×dₒ)/(dₒ-f) = (5×5.5)/(5.5-5) = 55 mm
      M₁ = -dᵢ₁/dₒ = -55/5.5 = -10
      For eyepiece: M₂ = 25/fe = 25/2.5 = 10
      Total angular magnification = M₁×M₂ = -10×10 = -100
   b) dₒ = f = 5.0 mm

9. a) Light from above the water undergoes total internal reflection when incident at angles greater than the critical angle.
   b) Angular diameter = 2×θc = 2×48.8° = 97.6°

10. a) Mirror image: 1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ → 1/10 = 1/15 + 1/dᵢ → dᵢ = 30 cm (to right of mirror)
       This becomes object for lens at distance 40-30 = 10 cm to left of lens
       Lens image: 1/20 = 1/10 + 1/dᵢ → dᵢ = 20 cm (to right of lens)
       Magnification by mirror: M₁ = -dᵢ/dₒ = -30/15 = -2
       Height after mirror = -2 × 3 cm = -6 cm
       Magnification by lens: M₂ = -20/10 = -2
       Final height = -2 × (-6) cm = 12 cm
    b) [Ray diagram would be drawn here]
    c) Real, upright (double inversion)
