# Solutions Layout Test

## Test 1: Basic Layout

1. A 2.0 kg object experiences a force of 5.0 N. Calculate the acceleration.

<div class='solution-grid'>
   <div class='sigfig'>
   3 significant digits
   </div>
   <div class='parent-formula'>
   
   $F = m \cdot a$
   </div>
   <div class='givens'>
   
   $m = 2.0 \, \mathrm{kg}$<br>
   $F = 5.0 \, \mathrm{N}$
   </div>
   <div class='calculations'>
   
   $a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{5.0 \, \mathrm{N}}{2.0 \, \mathrm{kg}} = 2.5 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}$
   </div>
</div>

## Test 2: Complex Content

2. A projectile is launched at 30° with an initial velocity of 40 m/s. Calculate its maximum height and range.

<div class='solution-grid'>
   <div class='sigfig'>
   2 significant digits
   </div>
   <div class='parent-formula'>
   
   $y_{max} = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2 \theta}{2g}$<br>
   $R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$
   </div>
   <div class='givens'>
   
   $v_0 = 40 \, \mathrm{m/s}$<br>
   $\theta = 30°$<br>
   $g = 9.8 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}$
   </div>
   <div class='calculations'>
   
   $y_{max} = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2 \theta}{2g} = \frac{(40 \, \mathrm{m/s})^2 \sin^2 30°}{2 \cdot 9.8 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}} = \frac{1600 \cdot 0.25}{19.6} = \frac{400}{19.6} = 20 \, \mathrm{m}$<br><br>
   $R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin 2\theta}{g} = \frac{(40 \, \mathrm{m/s})^2 \sin 60°}{9.8 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}} = \frac{1600 \cdot 0.866}{9.8} = \frac{1386}{9.8} = 140 \, \mathrm{m}$
   </div>
</div>

## Test 3: No Parent Formula

3. A car accelerates from 0 to 60 mph in 8.0 seconds. Calculate its acceleration.

<div class='solution-grid'>
   <div class='sigfig'>
   2 significant digits
   </div>
   <div class='givens'>
   
   $v_i = 0 \, \mathrm{m/s}$<br>
   $v_f = 60 \, \mathrm{mph} = 26.8 \, \mathrm{m/s}$<br>
   $t = 8.0 \, \mathrm{s}$
   </div>
   <div class='calculations'>

$a = \frac{v_f - v_i}{t} = \frac{26.8 \, \mathrm{m/s} - 0 \, \mathrm{m/s}}{8.0 \, \mathrm{s}} = 3.4 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}$
</div>
</div>
