# Simple Circuit Problems

## Problem Set 9.2: Electricity Fundamentals

### Problems

1. A charge of +3.0 μC is placed at a distance of 5.0 cm from a charge of -2.0 μC. Calculate the electric potential energy between them if the potential difference is 12 V.

2. A battery provides a potential difference of 9.0 V and has a total charge of 3600 C. Calculate the energy stored in the battery.

3. A circuit has a current of 0.5 A flowing through it for 20 seconds. Calculate the total charge that passes through the circuit, in coulombs.

4. A resistor has a resistance of 10 ohms and a current of 2 A flowing through it. Calculate the voltage drop across the resistor.

5. If a potential difference of 12 V is applied across a resistor, and the current through it is 3 A, what is the resistance of the resistor?

6. A resistor has a resistance of 100.0 ohms and is connected to a battery with a potential difference of 12 V. Calculate the current flowing through the resistor.

7. A light bulb operates at a voltage of 120 V and has a resistance of 240 ohms. Calculate the power consumed by the bulb.

8. A heater draws a current of 5 A when connected to a voltage source of 120 V. Calculate the power consumed by the heater.

### Answers

1. 36 μJ
2. 32,400 J
3. 10 C
4. 20 V
5. 4 Ω
6. 0.12 A
7. 60 W
8. 600 W
