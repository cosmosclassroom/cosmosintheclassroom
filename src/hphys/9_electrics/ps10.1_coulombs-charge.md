# PS 10.1 | Coulomb’s Law and Charge


Problems
A balloon rubbed against denim gains a charge of -8.0 µC. What is the electric force between the balloon and the denim when the two are separated by a distance of 5.0 cm? 
Two identical conducting spheres are placed with their centers 0.30 m apart. One is given a charge of +12 x 10-9 C and the other is given a charge of -18 x 10-9 C.
Find the electric force exerted on one sphere by the other.
The spheres are connected by a conducting wire. After equilibrium (of charge) is reached, find the electric force between the two spheres
Two electrostatic point charges of +60.0 µC and +50.0 µC exert a repulsive force on each other of 175 N. What is the distance between the two charges?
A charge of +2.00 x 10-9 C is placed at the origin, and another charge of +4.00 x 10-9 C is placed at x=1.5 m. Find the point between these two charges where a charge, q3 of +3.00 x 10-9 C should be placed such that the net electric force on q3 is zero.
A charge q1 of -5.00 x 10-9 C and a charge q2 of -2.00 x 10-9 C are separated by a distance of 40.0 cm. Find the equilibrium position for a third charge of +15.0 x 10-9 C. 
An electron is released above the Earth’s surface. A second electron directly below it exerts just enough electric force on the first electron to cancel the gravitational force on it. The mass of an electron is 9.109 x 10-31 kg. Calculate the distance at which the electrical force cancels the gravitational force acting upon it.
A proton and an electron in a hydrogen atom are separated, on average, by a distance of 5.3 x 10-11 m. What is the magnitude and direction of the electric field set up by a proton at the position of the electron?
An electric field of 2.0 x 104 N/C is directed along the positive x-axis.
What is the electric force on an electron in this field?
What is the electric force on a proton in this field?
