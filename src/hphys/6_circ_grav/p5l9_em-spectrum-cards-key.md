| Name        | Wavelength  | Frequency  | Sources                                   | Uses                                      | Interactions with Matter                      |
|------------|------------|------------|-------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Radio**   | Longest    | Lowest     | - Lightning, Stars, and planets         | - MRI, Radio, and TV broadcasting       | - Passes through atmosphere, Can induce current in conductors |
| **Microwaves** | Shorter  | Higher     | - Specialty vacuum tubes, Stars, and planets | - Cooking, Satellite communications, Radar | - Can penetrate some materials, Absorbed by water molecules |
| **Infrared** | Even shorter | Even higher | - Hot objects, Stars, and planets      | - Thermal imaging, Remote controls, Heating | - Causes molecules to vibrate |
| **Visible**  | Short      | High       | - The Sun, Light bulbs, Stars           | - Seeing, Photography                    | - Depends on the material, Can be absorbed or reflected |
| **Ultraviolet** | Even shorter | Even higher | - Very hot objects, The Sun, Specialty lamps | - Sterilization, Tanning beds           | - Can cause ionization, Absorbed by some materials |
| **X-ray**   | Shorter    | Higher     | - Very hot objects, X-ray tubes, Stars   | - Medical imaging, Security scanning     | - Can penetrate soft tissues, Ionizing |
| **Gamma ray** | Shortest | Highest    | - Radioactive materials, Nuclear reactions, Stars | - Cancer treatment, Sterilization       | - Highly ionizing, Can penetrate most materials |
