# Stellar Evolution


The life cycle of stars follows predictable patterns:

$$L = 4\pi R^2\sigma T^4$$

Where:
- L is luminosity
- R is radius
- T is surface temperature

## Black Holes

The Schwarzschild radius is given by: 

$R_s = \frac{2GM}{c^2}$

Where:
- G is gravitational constant
- M is mass
- c is speed of light
