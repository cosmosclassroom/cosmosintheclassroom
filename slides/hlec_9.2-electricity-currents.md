# The “Life Force”

1791 \- Discovers that static electricity can cause a frog leg to twitch

![](assets\images\9_electricity\0_Lecture_11_Electrodynamics_1.png)

Twitching could also be induced by injecting a mixture of metals

Assumed the metals were conducting the “life force” of the animal

Luigi Galvani

1737 \- 1798

# Skeptical interrogation

Skeptical of Galvani’s “life force” claims

![](assets\images\9_electricity\0_Lecture_11_Electrodynamics_2.png)

Experiments showed that the metals were the source of the electricity\, conducted by the liquid in the frog tissue

Experimented with disks of zinc and silver to generate the first battery

Debunked the idea that electricity was only a property of living things

Alessandro Volta

1745 \- 1827

# Understanding Voltage

Voltage\, also known as electric potential  __difference__ \, is the measure of the potential energy  __difference __ per unit charge between  __two points __ in an electric field

It represents the  __work __ needed to  __move __ a  __charge __ from one point to another against an electric field

![](assets\images\9_electricity\0_Lecture_11_Electrodynamics_6.png)

Voltage is measured in  __volts \(V\)__

# Resistance and Resistors

__Resistance __ is a measure of how much a  __material opposes __ the flow of electric current

![](assets\images\9_electricity\0_Lecture_11_Electrodynamics_13.png)

__Resistors __ are passive  __electrical components __ designed to introduce resistance into an electric circuit

Visualize resistance as the narrowness of a pipe that  __restricts __ the  __flow __ of water\, or a thumb over a hose

# Measuring resistance

__Resistance \(R\)__  __ is a measure of how much a material opposes the flow of current\.__



![](assets\images\9_electricity\0_Lecture_11_Electrodynamics_14.png)

Resistance is measured in  __ohms $Ω$
$$R = \frac{1 V}{1 \,amp}$$
$resistance = \frac{voltage\,drop}{current}$


One ohm is the amount of resistance experienced by 1 amp of current when it experiences a voltage drop of 1 V

# Electrical Power

Electrical power is the rate at which electrical energy is transferred or converted into other forms of energy

Power in an electrical circuit is calculated as:

$P = V \cdot I$
$\text{power = voltage} \times \text{current}$

Power is measured in watts $W$

$1 watt = \frac{1 Joule}{s} = \frac{J}{s}$

$1 watt = volts \times amps $
