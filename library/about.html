About Cosmos in the Classroom

Exploring physics through interactive learning and discovery

Our Mission

Cosmos in the Classroom transforms physics education through innovative digital tools and interactive learning experiences. We believe that understanding the physical world should be engaging, accessible, and deeply meaningful for every student.

Our Learning Systems

🌌 Portal

Your gateway to structured physics curricula, research briefs, and personalized learning paths designed for both Honors and Standard physics students.

⚡ Chunker

AI-powered content analysis that breaks down complex physics concepts into digestible learning chunks, making difficult topics more approachable.

🧠 Socrates

Generate research briefs and engage in Socratic questioning to develop deeper understanding through guided inquiry and critical thinking.

📚 Library

Comprehensive physics resources, curated materials, and reference content to support your learning journey at every level.

Educational Philosophy

We embrace the Socratic method, encouraging students to discover physics principles through questioning, experimentation, and reflection. Our approach emphasizes:

  • Inquiry-Based Learning: Students develop understanding through guided questions and exploration
  • Progressive Scaffolding: Complex concepts build systematically from fundamental principles
  • Real-World Applications: Physics concepts connected to practical, everyday phenomena
  • Individual Pacing: Adaptive learning paths that meet students where they are

About the Creator

Jonathan, educator and physics enthusiast, designed this platform with the belief that every student can develop a deep appreciation for the physical world. With years of classroom experience, he understands the challenges students face and has crafted these tools to make physics both accessible and inspiring.
