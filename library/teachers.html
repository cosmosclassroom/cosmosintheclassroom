For Teachers

Resources and guidance for educators using Cosmos in the Classroom

Platform Overview

Cosmos in the Classroom provides a comprehensive suite of tools designed to enhance physics education through interactive learning experiences. Our platform supports both Honors Physics and Standard Physics curricula with adaptive content and assessment tools.

Curriculum Structure

📚 Honors Physics

Advanced curriculum with 10 sequential units covering:

  • Principles of Physics
  • Kinematics & Dynamics
  • Energy & Momentum
  • Circular Motion & Gravitation
  • Simple Harmonic Motion
  • Wave Mechanics
  • Electrostatics & Circuits
  • Electromagnetic Radiation
  • Light & Optics
  • Modern Physics

🎯 Standard Physics

Accessible curriculum designed for broader student populations with emphasis on:

  • Conceptual understanding
  • Real-world applications
  • Hands-on activities
  • Progressive skill building
  • Multiple assessment formats

Teaching Tools

🌌 Portal System

Student Dashboard: Personalized learning paths and progress tracking

Teacher Features: Class management, assignment distribution, and analytics

Research Briefs: AI-generated content summaries and study guides

⚡ Chunker Engine

Content Analysis: Break complex topics into manageable segments

Adaptive Learning: Automatically adjust difficulty based on student needs

Prerequisite Mapping: Track conceptual dependencies

🧠 Socrates System

Question Generation: Create Socratic dialogues for any topic

Critical Thinking: Guide students through inquiry-based learning

Assessment Tools: Formative evaluation through questioning

Implementation Guide

1. Getting Started

  • Review curriculum alignment with your standards
  • Explore the Portal to understand navigation
  • Test the Chunker with sample content
  • Experiment with Socrates question generation

2. Classroom Integration

  • Introduce students to the Portal interface
  • Guide initial exploration of the Library
  • Demonstrate Socratic questioning techniques
  • Establish classroom protocols for system use

3. Ongoing Use

  • Monitor student progress through the Portal
  • Use research briefs for lesson planning
  • Generate questions for class discussions
  • Adapt content based on student feedback
