I

The Rules of the Game

Establishing the fundamental language and tools of observation.

Unit 1: Speaking the Same Language

Core Idea: Establishes a universal language for precise and honest measurement.

Key Skills: Dimensional analysis, unit conversions, significant figures.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Challenge of Mapping the World.

Crosscutting Lens:

Scale, Proportion, and Quantity.

Unit 2: The Art of Observation

Core Idea: Describes motion quantitatively using position, velocity, and acceleration.

Key Skills: Graphical analysis of motion, 1D kinematic equations.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Launch and Trajectory of a Sounding Rocket.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Cause & Effect, Scale, Proportion & Quantity, Systems & System Models.

Unit 3: The World in 3D

Core Idea: Analyzes complex 2D motion as the sum of simple, independent linear motions.

Key Skills: Vector decomposition, projectile motion, relative velocity.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The First Use of a Cannon at the Siege of Constantinople.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Structure & Function, Scale, Proportion & Quantity, Systems & System Models, Stability & Change.

II

The Great Unification

Discovering the universal laws that govern motion, energy, and interactions.

Unit 4: The Why of Motion

Core Idea: Force is the cause of changes in motion, governed by Newton's Laws.

Key Skills: Free-body diagrams, applying ΣF=ma, friction.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Construction of the Great Pyramid.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Stability & Change, Cause & Effect.

Unit 5: The Universe's Accounting

Core Idea: Tracks changes in systems using the concept of Energy and its conservation.

Key Skills: Calculating Work, Power, KE & PE, Conservation of Energy.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Power of the Hoover Dam.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Energy & Matter, Scale, Proportion & Quantity.

Unit 6: The Conversation of Matter

Core Idea: Analyzes interactions through the lens of Momentum and its conservation.

Key Skills: Calculating Momentum & Impulse, Conservation of Momentum.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The "Gravity Assist" of the Voyager Probes.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Systems & System Models, Energy & Matter, Cause & Effect.

Unit 7: The Great Cosmic Waltz

Core Idea: Applies force and motion principles to universal gravity and orbital mechanics.

Key Skills: Centripetal force, Universal Gravitation.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Design of a Geostationary Satellite.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Systems & System Models, Cause & Effect, Scale, Proportion & Quantity.

III

The Unseen World

Exploring the physics of waves, light, and the fundamental forces of matter.

Unit 8: The Traveling Ripple

Core Idea: Energy can be transferred through space and matter via waves.

Key Skills: Wave equation (v=fλ), wave interference analysis.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Sound-Dampening of a Modern Recording Studio.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Structure & Function, Cause & Effect, Systems & System Models.

Unit 9: The Story of Sight

Core Idea: Models the behavior of light as it reflects and refracts through mirrors and lenses.

Key Skills: Snell's Law, thin lens equation, ray diagrams.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Use of Corrective Lenses.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Structure & Function, Cause & Effect.

Unit 10: The Force That Binds Us All

Core Idea: Investigates the fundamental electrostatic force that governs the structure of matter.

Key Skills: Coulomb's Law for forces, Ohm's Law for circuits.

Anchoring Phenomenon:

The Physics of a Lightning Strike.

Crosscutting Lenses:

Energy & Matter, Cause & Effect.
