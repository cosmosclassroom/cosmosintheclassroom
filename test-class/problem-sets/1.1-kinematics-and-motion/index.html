HPHYS Unit 1

Difficulty:

Submission Format: paper

Estimated Time: 45 minutes

Topics: kinematics, motion, velocity, acceleration

Problem Set 1.1: Kinematics and Motion

Focus: Kinematics, velocity, acceleration

Prerequisites: Students should understand displacement, velocity, and acceleration before attempting this problem set.

Learning Objectives:

  • Calculate displacement, velocity, and acceleration
  • Interpret and construct motion graphs
  • Apply kinematic equations to solve problems

Instructions

  • Complete all work on separate paper or in your physics notebook
  • Show all work, including unit conversions and intermediate steps
  • Use appropriate significant figures throughout your calculations
  • Include proper units in all final answers

Part A: Foundational Skills

1. A runner travels 100 meters east in 12 seconds. What is her average velocity?

2. Convert 72 km/h to meters per second.

3. A car accelerates from rest to 20 m/s in 5 seconds. What is its acceleration?

Part B: Conceptual Understanding

4. Explain the difference between displacement and distance. Give an example.

5. Sketch a velocity-time graph for an object that starts at rest, accelerates uniformly, then moves at constant velocity.

Part C: Application and Synthesis

6. A ball is dropped from a height of 10 meters. How long does it take to reach the ground? (Use $g = 9.8\ \text{m/s}^2$)

7. A student walks 3 meters east, then 4 meters north. What is her total displacement?
