Unit 3: The Siege at Constantinople — Assessment Overview

This unit includes two quizzes and one comprehensive chapter assessment. Each is designed to evaluate your understanding of two-dimensional kinematics, projectile motion, and relative velocity in the context of historical siege engineering.

Quiz 1: Independence of Motion & Vector Components

  • Focus: Conceptual understanding of how two-dimensional motion can be analyzed as independent horizontal and vertical components.
  • Format: Multiple-choice and short-answer questions on vector decomposition, kinematic equations, and basic projectile motion.
  • Sample Topics:
    • Breaking vectors into components
    • Predicting projectile paths
    • Identifying independence of motion in real-world scenarios

Quiz 2: Relative Motion & Real-World Applications

  • Focus: Applying relative velocity concepts and solving problems involving moving objects and reference frames.
  • Format: Multiple-choice and calculation-based questions on relative velocity, moving targets, and practical applications in siege scenarios.
  • Sample Topics:
    • Calculating resultant velocity
    • Analyzing motion of objects in different reference frames
    • Predicting outcomes in pursuit and crossing problems

Chapter Assessment: The Siege of Constantinople

  • Focus: Synthesis and application of all major unit concepts in a historical engineering context.
  • Format: Multi-part test including conceptual questions, quantitative problem solving, and a synthesis challenge.
  • Sample Problems:
    • Analyzing projectile motion for siege engines
    • Solving for time of flight, range, and maximum height
    • Applying relative velocity to moving platforms
    • Advising on engineering solutions for historical scenarios
