Part 3: The Master’s Insight (Synthesis & Analysis) This problem requires you to synthesize multiple concepts to advise the Sultan himself.

  1. The great cannon, “The Basilica,” is mounted on a massive, movable siege tower that is being winched parallel to the city walls at a constant speed of 1.50 m/s. A key defensive tower is located 1600 m away (perpendicularly) from the siege tower’s path. The Basilica fires a cannonball with a muzzle velocity of 160.0 m/s. The cannoneer must aim the barrel both vertically (to achieve the correct range) and horizontally (to account for the siege tower’s motion).

Assume the cannoneer sets the vertical launch angle to 45.0° for maximum range.

a) Ignoring the siege tower’s motion for a moment, calculate the theoretical range of the cannonball if fired from a stationary position. Is this range sufficient to hit a target 1600 m away?

b) Using the given muzzle velocity and angle, calculate the total time of flight for the cannonball.

c) During this time of flight, the siege tower continues to move. To hit the stationary target, the cannoneer must aim the cannon barrel slightly backward (against the direction of motion).
