Quiz 3.2: Relative Motion & Real-World Applications

Instructions: Answer all questions. Show all work for calculations, including base formulas, units, and significant figures. You may use a calculator and reference sheet.

Part 1: Conceptual Understanding (Multiple Choice)

  1. If a boat moves at 4.0 m/s east in a river flowing at 2.0 m/s north, what is the boat’s speed relative to the ground? a) 2.0 m/s b) 4.0 m/s c) 6.0 m/s d) $\sqrt{20}$ m/s

  2. A bird flies at 5.0 m/s relative to the air, but the wind blows at 3.0 m/s in the same direction. What is the bird’s speed relative to the ground? a) 2.0 m/s b) 5.0 m/s c) 8.0 m/s d) Cannot be determined

  3. When solving relative motion problems, which principle is most important? a) Conservation of energy b) Independence of motion c) Vector addition d) Newton’s Third Law

Part 2: Short Answer

  1. Explain how reference frames affect the measurement of velocity in a real-world scenario (e.g., a train, river, or moving walkway).

  2. Describe a situation in which relative velocity must be considered to avoid a collision.

Part 3: Problem Solving

  1. A swimmer can swim at 1.5 m/s in still water. The river flows at 1.0 m/s. If the swimmer aims directly across a 60.0 m wide river, how long does it take to cross, and how far downstream does the swimmer land?

  2. A car travels north at 20.0 m/s. A motorcycle passes it going east at 15.0 m/s. What is the velocity of the motorcycle relative to the car (magnitude and direction)?

  3. A rescue drone must deliver supplies to a moving ship. The ship moves east at 8.0 m/s, and the drone flies at 12.0 m/s relative to the air. What heading should the drone take to reach the ship directly, and what is its velocity relative to the ground?

Part 4: Synthesis

  1. Describe a historical or engineering scenario (other than the siege) where relative motion analysis is essential. Explain how you would use vector addition to solve a practical problem in that context.
