[ ] Next steps are clearly communicated ``` ## Lecture Creation Guidelines ### Pedagogical Approach 1. **Investigation-First:** Always connect to what students have already discovered 2. **Guided Discovery:** Help students formalize their own observations 3. **Active Learning:** Include frequent opportunities for student thinking and discussion 4. **Just-in-Time:** Introduce formal concepts when students need them to make sense of data ### Mathematical Development - Build equations from conceptual understanding, not from memorization - Use multiple representations (graphs, diagrams, equations, words) - Connect mathematical relationships to physical meaning - Practice problem-solving strategies, not just calculation ### Student Engagement - Use student data and observations as starting points - Include think-pair-share and other active learning techniques - Connect to real-world applications that students find relevant - Provide immediate feedback on practice problems ### Assessment Integration - Include formative assessment opportunities throughout - Check for understanding before moving to new concepts - Address misconceptions as they arise - Use exit tickets to gauge overall comprehension ### Timing and Pacing - Plan for flexible timing based on student needs - Include natural break points for questions - Balance direct instruction with active learning - Leave time for practice and feedback
