Investigation 1 of 2

Overview

Abstract

This qualitative investigation uses simple, classic demonstrations (the coin-and-card drop and the tablecloth pull) to develop a conceptual understanding of inertia, the resistance of an object to any change in its state of motion, as described by Newton’s First Law.

Introduction

It might seem obvious that you have to push something to keep it moving, but that’s only because our world is filled with friction. Isaac Newton’s brilliant insight was to imagine a perfect, frictionless world. In that world, an object in motion stays in motion forever. This property of matter to resist changes in its motion is called inertia, and you can feel it every time a car suddenly stops or starts. This lab explores that feeling.
