Question 1 (5 points)

What is the SI base unit for length?

The meter is the SI base unit for length. Other units like kilometers and centimeters are derived from the meter.

Question 2 (10 points)

Select ALL vector quantities from the list below:

Vector quantities have both magnitude and direction. Velocity, displacement, and force are vectors. Speed and distance are scalar quantities (magnitude only).

Question 3 (10 points)

Convert 2.5 kilometers to meters. Enter your answer as a number.

m

To convert kilometers to meters, multiply by 1000. Therefore, 2.5 km × 1000 = 2500 m.
