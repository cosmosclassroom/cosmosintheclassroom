Problem Set 2.1: Interpreting Kinematic Graphs

Introduction

The data from a rocket launch is often visualized as a graph. In this problem set, you will practice reading the story of motion from position-time (p-t) and velocity-time (v-t) graphs, connecting the graphical patterns to the physical motion of our sounding rocket.

Key Formulas

  • For a position-time graph: Average Velocity = Slope = $\frac{\Delta\vec{x}}{\Delta t}$
  • For a velocity-time graph: Acceleration = Slope = $\frac{\Delta\vec{v}}{\Delta t}$
  • For a velocity-time graph: Displacement = Area under the curve

Global Standards

  • For all problems, assume the acceleration due to gravity is $\vec{a}_g = -9.80 \, m/s^2$.
  • Present all final answers with the correct units and number of significant figures.

Part D: Acceleration Analysis

7. A rocket’s velocity-time graph shows a constant increase from 0 m/s to 1000 m/s over 20.0 s. a) What is the rocket’s acceleration? b) What distance does the rocket travel in this time? c) If the rocket continues with the same acceleration, how long will it take to reach a velocity of 2000 m/s?

8. A cyclist travels with a velocity given by v(t) = 4t - 0.2t² m/s, where t is in seconds. a) Find the cyclist’s acceleration as a function of time. b) At what time is the velocity maximum? c) Draw the velocity-time graph from t = 0 s to t = 25.0 s. d) Calculate the total distance traveled in 25.0 s.

Part E: Real-World Applications

9. A train accelerates from rest at 0.50 m/s² for 60.0 s, then maintains a constant velocity for 120.0 s, and finally decelerates at 1.0 m/s² until it stops. a) Draw the velocity-time graph for the entire journey. b) Calculate the total distance traveled. c) What was the train’s maximum speed?

10. A rocket launches vertically with an acceleration of 15.0 m/s² for 20.0 s. Then its engines shut off and it continues to rise while decelerating due to gravity. a) Draw the velocity-time graph from launch until the rocket reaches its maximum height. b) What is the maximum height reached by the rocket? c) How long after launch does the rocket reach its maximum height?
