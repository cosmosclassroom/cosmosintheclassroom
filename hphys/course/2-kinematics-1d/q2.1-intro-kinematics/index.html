Question 1 (5 points)

Segment 1 of 4: Uniform Acceleration

Context: The test begins with the EV at rest. At t=0.00 s, the driver accelerates the vehicle uniformly. This initial phase tests the vehicle’s maximum power output and traction.

Assume the vehicle accelerates from rest at a constant rate of a = +6.00 m/s² for the first 5.00 s.

Question 1: Calculate the vehicle’s velocity, v_f, at the end of the 5.00 s interval.

m/s

Using v_f = v_i + at, with v_i = 0: v_f = 0 + (6.00 m/s²)(5.00 s) = 30.0 m/s

Question 2 (5 points)

Question 2: Calculate the vehicle’s displacement, Δx, during this acceleration phase.

m

Using Δx = v_i*t + ½at², with v_i = 0: Δx = 0 + ½(6.00 m/s²)(5.00 s)² = 75.0 m

Question 11 (5 points)

Segment 3 of 4: Uniform Braking

Context: In the final phase of the test, the driver applies the brakes to bring the vehicle to a complete stop, testing the performance of the braking system.

At t=15.00 s, the vehicle is traveling at v_i = +30.0 m/s. The driver applies the brakes, bringing the car to a stop (v_f = 0.0 m/s) over a distance of 75.0 m.

Question 1: Calculate the vehicle’s acceleration, a, during the braking phase. Is the sign of your answer consistent with the vehicle slowing down?

m/s²

Using v_f² = v_i² + 2aΔx: 0 = (30.0)² + 2a(75.0). Solving: a = -900/(150) = -6.0 m/s². Negative sign indicates deceleration.

Question 12 (5 points)

Question 2: Calculate the time, Δt, it takes for the vehicle to come to a complete stop.

s

Using v_f = v_i + at: 0 = 30.0 + (-6.0)t. Solving: t = 5.0 s
