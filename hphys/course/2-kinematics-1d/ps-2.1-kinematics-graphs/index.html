Problem Set 2.1: Interpreting Kinematic Graphs

HPHYS | Problem set | Unit 2

The data from a rocket launch is often visualized as a graph. In this problem set, you will practice reading the story of motion from position-time (p-t) and velocity-time (v-t) graphs, connecting the graphical patterns to the physical motion of our sounding rocket.

Key Formulae

  • For a position-time graph: Average Velocity = Slope = $\frac{\Delta\vec{x}}{\Delta t}$
  • For a velocity-time graph: Acceleration = Slope = $\frac{\Delta\vec{v}}{\Delta t}$
  • For a velocity-time graph: Displacement = Area under the curve

Standards for All Problems

  • For all problems, assume the acceleration due to gravity is $\vec{a}_g = -9.80 \, m/s^2$.
  • Present all final answers with the correct units and number of significant figures.

Part A: Reading Position-Time Graphs

Problem A.1.1 Apprentice

A car’s position is recorded every second, giving the following position-time data:

0 0
1 5
2 20
3 45
4 80

a) Plot the position-time graph. b) Calculate the average velocity for each time interval. c) At what time was the car moving the fastest? Explain your reasoning.

Part B: Reading Velocity-Time Graphs

Problem B.1.3 Apprentice

A runner’s velocity-time graph is shown below:

[Graph description: Velocity-time graph showing a horizontal line at 5 m/s from 0-10s, then a linear decrease to 0 m/s at 20s, then a horizontal line at 0 m/s until 30s]

Part C: Connecting Position and Velocity

Problem C.1.5 Journeyman

[Graph description: Velocity-time graph showing a straight line with positive slope from the origin to (10s, 20 m/s)]

Problem C.1.6 Journeyman

Part D: Acceleration Analysis

Problem D.7 Journeyman

A rocket’s velocity-time graph shows a constant increase from 0 m/s to 1000 m/s over 20.0 s. a) What is the rocket’s acceleration? b) What distance does the rocket travel in this time? c) If the rocket continues with the same acceleration, how long will it take to reach a velocity of 2000 m/s?

Problem D.8 Master

A cyclist travels with a velocity given by v(t) = 4t - 0.2t² m/s, where t is in seconds. a) Find the cyclist’s acceleration as a function of time. b) At what time is the velocity maximum? c) Draw the velocity-time graph from t = 0 s to t = 25.0 s. d) Calculate the total distance traveled in 25.0 s.

Part E: Real-World Applications

Problem E.9 Journeyman

A train accelerates from rest at 0.50 m/s² for 60.0 s, then maintains a constant velocity for 120.0 s, and finally decelerates at 1.0 m/s² until it stops. a) Draw the velocity-time graph for the entire journey. b) Calculate the total distance traveled. c) What was the train’s maximum speed?

Problem E.10 Master

A rocket launches vertically with an acceleration of 15.0 m/s² for 20.0 s. Then its engines shut off and it continues to rise while decelerating due to gravity. a) Draw the velocity-time graph from launch until the rocket reaches its maximum height. b) What is the maximum height reached by the rocket? c) How long after launch does the rocket reach its maximum height?
