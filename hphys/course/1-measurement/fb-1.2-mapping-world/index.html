Mapping the World

<h2>A New Tool: Describing Direction</h2>
<p>So far, we have learned how to answer the question, “How much?” But in our quest to map the world, this is only half the story. To give directions, you need to know more than just how far to walk; you need to know which way to walk. Science makes a crucial distinction between scalars and vectors. A distance of 5 kilometers is a scalar, while a displacement of 5 kilometers to the northeast is a vector. Check for Understanding: Identify each of the following as a scalar or a vector and explain why.</p>
