Section 1, Q1: Convert 2.45 km to meters, showing proper significant figures.

There are 1000 meters in 1 kilometer. 2.45 km * (1000 m / 1 km) = 2450 m. The input has 3 significant figures, so the output should as well.

Question 3 (4 points)

Section 2, Q3: A displacement vector d has a magnitude of 15.0 m at an angle of 37° north of east. Describe a neat sketch of this vector with labeled horizontal (dx) and vertical (dy) components, including a right triangle and the angle.

Question 4 (6 points)

Section 2, Q4: For the vector described in Q3, compute the eastward component, dx. Show your formula and substitution.

m

The eastward component is the adjacent side. dx = d * cos(θ) = 15.0 m * cos(37°) ≈ 11.98 m, which rounds to 12.0 m with 3 significant figures.

Question 5 (7 points)

Section 2, Q5: For the vector described in Q3, compute the northward component, dy.

m

The northward component is the opposite side. dy = d * sin(θ) = 15.0 m * sin(37°) ≈ 9.027 m, which rounds to 9.03 m with 3 significant figures.
