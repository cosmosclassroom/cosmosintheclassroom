About This Course

Honors Physics uses a phenomenon-based approach where you'll explore real-world events and develop mathematical models to explain them. Each unit is anchored by an authentic phenomenon that drives your learning through investigations, labs, and problem-solving.

What You'll Find Here:

  • Lectures: Detailed explanations and worked examples
  • Hexagon Labs: Hands-on investigations with real data
  • Problem Sets: Practice problems organized by difficulty level
  • Assessments: Unit assessments and progress checks
  • Flexbooks: Interactive reading materials and resources
