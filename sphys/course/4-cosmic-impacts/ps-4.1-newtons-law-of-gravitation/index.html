Problem Set 4.1: Newton's Law of Gravitation

SPHYS • Problem set • Unit 4
▶ Instructions

The path of the Chelyabinsk meteor, like the orbit of every planet and moon, is governed by Newton’s Universal Law of Gravitation. In this problem set, you will apply this fundamental law to calculate the invisible forces that shape our solar system, moving from the pull between planets to the effect of distance on an asteroid’s trajectory.

▶ Standards for All Problems
