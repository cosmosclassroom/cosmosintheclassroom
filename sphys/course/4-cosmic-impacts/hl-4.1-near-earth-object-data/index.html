Overview

Abstract

This investigation places students in the role of a planetary defense scientist. Students will analyze a provided dataset of real and simulated Near-Earth Objects (NEOs) to assess their potential risk to Earth. They will sort the data by key risk factors—size, velocity, and proximity—to determine which objects pose the greatest threat.

Introduction

The Chelyabinsk meteor was a terrifying reminder that our solar system is a busy, dynamic place. Astronomers are constantly monitoring thousands of objects whose orbits bring them close to Earth. Your task is to act as an analyst at NASA’s Planetary Defense Coordination Office. You will use a provided dataset to assess the potential risk of some of these objects and decide which ones we need to watch most closely.

Investigation 1: Triage for Planet Earth

Objective: Analyze orbital data of known NEOs to classify their risk level based on size, velocity, and proximity to Earth’s orbit.

Data Source: Provided dataset of known NEOs.
