A New Lens: Energy in an Orbit

Re-examine the concept of an orbit. In the Flexbook, we described it as a balance between forward motion and gravitational pull. Now, describe an elliptical orbit through the lens of **Energy and Matter**. How is the total energy of the system conserved as the orbiting object trades kinetic energy for potential energy?

Phase 3: Reflection

Our investigation into cosmic impacts is complete. The final step is to construct your definitive, evidence-based explanation of orbital mechanics, and then to reflect on your own learning process.

Final Synthesis: The Clockwork Cosmos

This is your final, complete explanation of how the principles of gravitation and orbital mechanics govern our solar system. Explain how Newton's Universal Law of Gravitation creates the stable (but sometimes intersecting) orbital paths described by Kepler's Laws. Use specific evidence from your labs and key concepts from the Flexbook to explain why cosmic collisions are not random events, but predictable consequences of this celestial clockwork.

Final Model: Planetary Defense

Accompany your synthesis with a final model. Design a hypothetical planetary defense mission. Draw a diagram showing the Earth, an approaching asteroid, and a spacecraft designed to deflect it. Explain what physics principle your deflection method uses (e.g., kinetic impactor, gravity tractor) and at what point in the asteroid's orbit it would be most effective to intervene.
