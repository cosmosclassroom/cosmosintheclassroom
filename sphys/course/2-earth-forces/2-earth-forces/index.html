This is your final, complete explanation of the 2005 Afar Rift. Your narrative must weave together all the core concepts of this unit into a coherent story. Explain how the microscopic process of radioactive decay leads to the macroscopic engine of mantle convection, and how this engine, governed by Newton's Laws of Motion, created the immense, unbalanced tensional forces necessary to overcome inertia and friction to tear the continent apart.
