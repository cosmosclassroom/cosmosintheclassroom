## Unit Reflection Questions
After completing all activities, reflect on these big picture questions:

1. How can we design more reliable systems to meet our communities' energy needs?
2. What are the most important trade-offs when designing an energy grid?
3. How do energy policy decisions affect different communities?
4. Why can't we easily store electricity for emergencies?
5. What did you learn about the connection between engineering design and social justice?
