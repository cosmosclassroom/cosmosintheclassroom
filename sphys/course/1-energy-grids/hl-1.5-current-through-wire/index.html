Abstract

In this investigation, you will use a simulation to explore how physical properties of a wire—such as its length, thickness, temperature, and material—influence electric current and energy transfer. You will design and conduct your own controlled experiment to isolate one variable and measure its effect, mirroring the process scientists use to understand complex systems.

Introduction

Electricity flows through conductors like copper wires because electrons are free to move. But is that flow always the same? What factors might make it harder or easier for the current to flow, and how does that affect the efficiency of energy transfer? This lab connects the microscopic world of electrons moving in a wire to the macroscopic challenge of building efficient energy systems.
