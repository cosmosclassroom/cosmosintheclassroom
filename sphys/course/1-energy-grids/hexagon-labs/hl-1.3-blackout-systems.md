## Part 3: The Big Picture - Regional Power Grid

### **Data Set C: Texas Power Grid Components**

Here are the major parts of the Texas power grid and their typical voltages:

| Grid Component | Voltage Level | Purpose |
|:---------------|:--------------|:--------|
| Power plants | 13,000 V | Generate electricity |
| Transmission lines (long distance) | 345,000 V | Carry power across Texas |
| Distribution substations | 7,200 V | Reduce voltage for neighborhoods |
| Local transformers | 240 V | Final voltage reduction |
| Your house | 120 V | Safe voltage for appliances |

**Step 5:** Create a flow chart showing how electricity moves from power plant to your house. Include all five components and their voltage levels.

**Step 6:** Why do you think the transmission lines use such high voltage (345,000 V) when your house only needs 120 V?
