Research Brief P.1 Blueprint: Modeling Grid Reliability

The Engineering Challenge

You are part of a consulting team hired by your state’s Public Utility Commission following a major power grid failure. Your task is to develop a comprehensive system model that explains why the grid failed and propose evidence-based recommendations for preventing future blackouts while ensuring equitable access to reliable power.

Central Research Question

How can we model the electrical grid as a complex system to identify failure points, assess reliability trade-offs, and design solutions that serve all communities equitably?
