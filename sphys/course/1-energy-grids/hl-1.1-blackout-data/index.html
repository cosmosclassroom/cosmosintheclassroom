Abstract

In this investigation, you will act as a data analyst for an energy commission. Your task is to analyze a dataset of energy supply, energy demand, and temperature from the week of the February 2021 Texas power crisis. By visualizing and interpreting this data, you will identify the critical tipping point of the grid failure and construct an evidence-based explanation for why the blackout occurred.

Introduction

On February 15, 2021, the Texas energy grid experienced a catastrophic failure, leaving millions without power during a historic cold snap. This was not a single event, but a complex system failure. News reports blamed various factors, from frozen wind turbines to failures in the natural gas supply. To find the real story, we must look at the data. Your job is to be a data detective, using the provided evidence to piece together the sequence of events that led to the crisis.

Connection to Unit Phenomenon

This lab is a direct investigation of our unit’s anchoring phenomenon. The data shows that the Texas power crisis was a classic example of a complex system failure. While the failure of supply (frozen gas wells and wind turbines) was a major factor, it was the unprecedented surge in demand caused by the extreme cold that pushed the system over the edge. This highlights the central engineering challenge of grid design: you must build a system that can not only meet average demand but can also withstand the most extreme conditions it is ever likely to face.
