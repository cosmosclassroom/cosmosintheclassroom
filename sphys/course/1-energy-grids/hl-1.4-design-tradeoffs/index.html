Investigation 1 of 2

Overview

Abstract

This investigation uses a decision matrix approach to evaluate three energy source options for a community recovering from the 2021 Texas power crisis. Students will analyze quantitative data across multiple criteria (cost, land use, reliability, emissions) and use weighted scoring to determine the optimal solution for different community types with varying priorities.

Introduction

Today’s Question: How do engineers use data to make difficult decisions when there is no single “best” choice?

After the 2021 power crisis, many Texas communities realized they needed more reliable energy. Imagine your town must choose a new power plant. As engineers, your team must analyze the data and recommend a solution. This process always involves balancing competing priorities, or trade-offs.

Complete this lab in your Science Notebook.

Use a weighted decision matrix to quantitatively evaluate which energy source best fits your assigned community’s priorities.
