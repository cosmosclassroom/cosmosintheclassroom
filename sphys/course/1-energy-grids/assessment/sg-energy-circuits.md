## **Circuit Calculations (Mathematical Thinking)**

### Key Formulas:
- **Ohm's Law**: V = IR (Voltage = Current × Resistance)
- **Power equations**: P = VI 
- **Energy equation**: E = P × Δt (Energy = Power × Time)

*Note: Units will be provided for each variable on the assessment*

### What You Should Be Able to Do:
- **Solve for any variable** in V = IR when given the other two
- **Calculate electrical power** using P = VI
- **Calculate energy consumption** in kilowatt-hours
- **Show your work clearly** with units in every step
- **Choose the right equation** for each type of problem

### Study Prompts:
- Practice problems from Problem Set 1 - redo any you got wrong
- Make sure you can solve for V, I, or R in any V = IR problem
- Practice converting between W and kW, and between hours and other time units
- Check: Can you calculate your monthly energy bill for a specific appliance?
