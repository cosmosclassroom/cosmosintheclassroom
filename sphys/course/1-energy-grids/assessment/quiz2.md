## Part B: Calculations & Application (15 pts, ~12 min)

**4. Ohm's Law Practice (6 pts)**  
A lamp has a resistance of $24\,\Omega$ and is plugged into a $120\,\text{V}$ outlet.  
a) What is the current? Show your work.  
b) What does this current mean for the lamp?

**5. Power Calculation (5 pts)**  
A space heater uses $1,500\,\text{W}$ of power at $120\,\text{V}$.  
a) How much current does it draw? Show your work.  
b) Why is it important to know this current for safety?

**6. Energy Use (4 pts)**  
A $60\,\text{W}$ bulb runs for $3$ hours.  
a) How much energy does it use in Joules?  
b) Show your calculation steps.

**Key Terms Bank:**  
- **Voltage:** The push that moves electric current.  
- **Current:** The flow of electric charge.  
- **Resistance:** How much a material slows down current.  
- **Power:** How fast energy is used.  
- **Energy:** The ability to do work.
