Overview

Abstract

This investigation places students in the role of a data scientist to investigate the anchoring phenomenon. Students will analyze a multi-decade dataset of traffic collision data to identify and explain the puzzling trend: while cars have become safer and fatalities are decreasing, the total number of collisions is rising.

Introduction

We begin this unit with a modern-day puzzle from our daily lives. Common sense suggests that as cars get safer (with airbags, better brakes, etc.), the roads should become safer overall. But the data shows something strange: decreasing fatalities but increasing collisions and injuries. Your task is to be a data scientist and investigate this large dataset to develop a hypothesis that explains this puzzling trend.

Investigation 1: Decoding the Collision Puzzle

Objective: Analyze a dataset of traffic incidents to identify and explain the puzzling trend.

Data Source: Provided dataset summarizing US traffic statistics from 1990 to 2020.

In your log, propose a claim (a hypothesis) that explains the overall puzzling pattern. Your claim should answer both parts of the puzzle:
