▶ Instructions

To investigate the puzzling patterns in traffic data, we must first learn to precisely describe motion. Motion graphs are a physicist’s most powerful tool for visualizing the story of an object’s movement. In this problem set, you will practice interpreting these graphs to determine an object’s position, velocity, and acceleration, building the skills needed to analyze a vehicle collision.

▶ Key Formulae
  • Average Velocity: \(\vec{v}_{avg} = \frac{\Delta \vec{x}}{\Delta t} = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} \text{ (slope of position-time graph)}\)
  • Average Acceleration: \(\vec{a}_{avg} = \frac{\Delta \vec{v}}{\Delta t} = \frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}} \text{ (slope of velocity-time graph)}\)
  • Displacement: \(\Delta \vec{x} = \text{Area under velocity-time graph}\)
▶ Standards for All Problems
  • Clearly state what the slope and area represent for each type of graph.
  • Present all final answers with the correct units and to three significant figures.
