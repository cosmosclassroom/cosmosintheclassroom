Phase 3: Reflection

Our investigation into the traffic safety paradox is complete. The final step is to construct your definitive, evidence-based explanation, and then to reflect on your own learning process.

Final Synthesis: Solving the Paradox

This is your final, complete explanation of the traffic safety paradox. Your narrative must explain both why fatalities are down AND why collisions are up. Ground your explanation in the physics principles you have learned (Impulse-Momentum Theorem, kinematics, conservation of momentum) and the evidence from your Hexagon Labs.

Final Model: A Safer System

Accompany your synthesis with a final model. Draw a diagram of a 'Safe Transportation System.' Your model should include not only vehicle safety features (like a crumple zone) but also road design features (like a roundabout) and policy/driver behavior elements that work together to reduce both the frequency and severity of collisions.
