  • problem_number: 1 tier: level: 1 title: “The Apprentice’s Practice: Basic Wave Equation” phenomenon_connection: “This calculation determines the physical size of the waves used in our anchoring phenomenon, the microwave oven.” problem_statement: “A standard microwave oven operates at a frequency of 2.45 GHz ($2.45 \times 10^9 \, \text{Hz}$). What is the wavelength of these microwaves?” solution: givens: - “$f = 2.45 \times 10^9 \, \text{Hz}$” - “$v = c = 3.00 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}$” unknowns: - “$\lambda = ?$” steps: - “1. Select Parent Equation: The wave equation is $v = f \lambda$.” - “2. Algebraic Rearrangement: To solve for wavelength, divide both sides by frequency: $\lambda = \frac{v}{f}$.” - “3. Substitute Values and Track Units:” - | \(\\lambda = \\frac{3.00 \\times 10^8 \\, m/s}{2.45 \\times 10^9 \\, Hz} = \\frac{3.00 \\times 10^8 \\, m/s}{2.45 \\times 10^9 \\, 1/s}\) - | \(\\lambda = 0.122 \\, m\) final_answer: “The wavelength of the microwaves is 0.122 m (or 12.2 cm).”
  • problem_number: 2 tier: level: 2 title: “The Journeyman’s Task: Finding Frequency” phenomenon_connection: “Different technologies use different parts of the EM spectrum. This problem calculates the frequency of a common radio wave, allowing comparison to the microwaves in our oven.” problem_statement: “An FM radio station broadcasts at a wavelength of 3.20 meters. What is the broadcast frequency in Hertz (Hz)?” solution: givens: - “$\lambda = 3.20 \, \text{m}$” - “$v = c = 3.00 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}$” unknowns: - “$f = ?$” steps: - “1. Select Parent Equation: $v = f \lambda$.” - “2. Algebraic Rearrangement: To solve for frequency, divide both sides by wavelength: $f = \frac{v}{\lambda}$.” - “3. Substitute Values:” - | \(f = \\frac{3.00 \\times 10^8 \\, m/s}{3.20 \\, m} = 9.375 \\times 10^7 \\, \\text{Hz}\) final_answer: “The broadcast frequency is 9.38e7 Hz (or 93.8 MHz).”

  • problem_number: 3 tier: level: 3 title: “The Master’s Insight: Light Travel Time” phenomenon_connection: “While EM waves seem instantaneous on Earth, their finite speed is critical for technologies like GPS and for understanding astronomical observations.” problem_statement: “A red laser pointer emits light with a wavelength of 650. nm ($6.50 \times 10^{-7} \, \text{m}$).\na) What is the frequency of this light?\nb) How long does it take for this light to travel from Earth to the Moon, a distance of $3.84 \times 10^8 \, \text{m}$?” solution: givens: - “$\lambda = 6.50 \times 10^{-7} \, \text{m}$” - “$c = 3.00 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}$” - “$d = 3.84 \times 10^8 \, \text{m}$” unknowns: - “$f = ?$” - “$t = ?$” steps: - |
