Problem Set 5.2: Constructing the EM Spectrum

SPHYS | Problem set | Unit 5
▶ Instructions

The energy of an electromagnetic wave is carried in discrete packets called photons. The energy of a single photon determines how that wave interacts with matter, which is the key to understanding the safety of technologies like 5G. In this problem set, you will calculate photon energies across the spectrum to build a quantitative model that distinguishes between ionizing and non-ionizing radiation.

▶ Key Formulae
  • Photon Energy: \(E = hf\)
  • Wave Speed: \(c = f \lambda\)
▶ Standards for All Problems
  • Use the speed of light, $c = 3.00 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}$.
  • Use Planck’s Constant, $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34} \, \text{J} \cdot \text{s}$.
  • Present all final answers with the correct units and to three significant figures.
