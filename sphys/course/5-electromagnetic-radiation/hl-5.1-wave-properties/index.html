LAB OVERVIEW

——————————————————————————

overview: abstract: | This investigation uses data from a simulated wave experiment to discover the fundamental mathematical rule that governs all waves. Students will analyze a dataset of frequency and wavelength values to derive the wave speed equation ($v = f \lambda$).

introduction: | All waves, from the ripples in a pond to the light from a star, share fundamental properties. Long before we could see or measure light waves, scientists studying sound and water waves discovered a simple rule that connects a wave’s frequency, its wavelength, and its speed. Your task is to re-discover this simple, powerful rule from a set of experimental data.

background_context: | Before starting this lab, you should understand: - Frequency ($f$): The number of waves that pass a fixed point in one second. Measured in Hertz (Hz). - Wavelength ($\lambda$): The distance between two consecutive points on a wave (e.g., crest to crest). Measured in meters (m). - Wave Speed ($v$): How fast the wave disturbance travels through the medium. Measured in meters per second (m/s).
