Introduction

All waves, from the ripples in a pond to the light from a distant star, share a set of common properties and obey the same simple rules. Before we can understand the complex nature of the electromagnetic radiation used in a microwave oven, we must first uncover this fundamental rulebook. Your task is to analyze the data from a simple wave simulation to find the hidden pattern connecting a wave’s properties.

Connection to Unit Phenomenon

This lab reveals the fundamental rule that governs the invisible waves inside a microwave oven. The equation you derived, $v = f \lambda$, applies to all electromagnetic waves, which all travel at the speed of light (c). Therefore, for microwaves, the equation becomes $c = f \lambda$. This means that a microwave’s frequency and its wavelength are precisely and inversely related. Engineers chose a specific frequency (and therefore a specific wavelength) for microwave ovens because it is particularly effective at transferring energy to water molecules. Understanding this fundamental wave equation is the first step to understanding how that energy transfer works.
