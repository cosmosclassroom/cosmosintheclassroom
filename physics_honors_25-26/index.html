Physics Honors 25-26

Welcome to Physics Honors, an advanced high school physics course designed to develop sophisticated understanding of physical principles through mathematical modeling, experimental investigation, and scientific reasoning.

Course Overview

This course emphasizes:

  • Mathematical Rigor: Complete derivations and algebraic problem-solving
  • Experimental Design: Data-driven investigations with uncertainty analysis
  • 3D Learning: Integration of Science and Engineering Practices, Disciplinary Core Ideas, and Crosscutting Concepts
  • Real-World Applications: Connections to current research and engineering challenges

Course Units

Prologue

Introduction to physics thinking, measurement, and scientific methodology

Unit 1: Measurement and Uncertainty

  • Core Topics: Significant figures, dimensional analysis, error propagation
  • Key Skills: Precision vs. accuracy, mathematical modeling fundamentals
  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

Unit 4: Dynamics

  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

Content Types Explained

📚 Flexbook Chapters

Comprehensive textbook-style content with Burke-Sagan narrative voice, mathematical derivations, and real-world connections.

🔬 Hexagon Labs

Data-driven investigations emphasizing experimental design, error analysis, and quantitative reasoning.

📝 Problem Sets

Tiered mathematical challenges progressing from direct application to open-ended design problems.

🔍 Research Briefs

Metacognitive activities connecting physics concepts to current research and technological applications.

🎯 Lectures

Slide presentations with mathematical rigor, historical context, and conceptual development.

Academic Standards

This course maintains honors-level expectations through:

  • Mathematical Rigor: Algebraic derivations, dimensional analysis, significant figures
  • Vector Notation: Proper use of $\vec{F}$, $\vec{v}$, $\vec{a}$ throughout
  • Unit Consistency: Metric system with units included in all mathematical steps
  • Scientific Communication: Written explanations of mathematical reasoning

Assessment Philosophy

Assessment integrates formative and summative approaches:

  • Conceptual Understanding: Prediction-observation-explanation sequences
  • Mathematical Fluency: Multi-step problems requiring synthesis
  • Experimental Skills: Design, data analysis, and uncertainty evaluation
  • Communication: Scientific writing and mathematical justification

This course prepares students for AP Physics examinations and college-level physics coursework through rigorous mathematical treatment and authentic scientific practices.
