Solutions Layout Test

Test 1: Basic Layout

  1. A 2.0 kg object experiences a force of 5.0 N. Calculate the acceleration.

3 significant digits

$F = m \cdot a$

$m = 2.0 \, \mathrm{kg}$
$F = 5.0 \, \mathrm{N}$

$a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{5.0 \, \mathrm{N}}{2.0 \, \mathrm{kg}} = 2.5 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}$

Test 2: Complex Content

  1. A projectile is launched at 30° with an initial velocity of 40 m/s. Calculate its maximum height and range.

2 significant digits

$y_{max} = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2 \theta}{2g}$
$R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$

$v_0 = 40 \, \mathrm{m/s}$
$\theta = 30°$
$g = 9.8 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}$

$y_{max} = \frac{v_0^2 \sin^2 \theta}{2g} = \frac{(40 \, \mathrm{m/s})^2 \sin^2 30°}{2 \cdot 9.8 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}} = \frac{1600 \cdot 0.25}{19.6} = \frac{400}{19.6} = 20 \, \mathrm{m}$

$R = \frac{v_0^2 \sin 2\theta}{g} = \frac{(40 \, \mathrm{m/s})^2 \sin 60°}{9.8 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}} = \frac{1600 \cdot 0.866}{9.8} = \frac{1386}{9.8} = 140 \, \mathrm{m}$

Test 3: No Parent Formula

  1. A car accelerates from 0 to 60 mph in 8.0 seconds. Calculate its acceleration.

2 significant digits

$v_i = 0 \, \mathrm{m/s}$
$v_f = 60 \, \mathrm{mph} = 26.8 \, \mathrm{m/s}$
$t = 8.0 \, \mathrm{s}$

$a = \frac{v_f - v_i}{t} = \frac{26.8 \, \mathrm{m/s} - 0 \, \mathrm{m/s}}{8.0 \, \mathrm{s}} = 3.4 \, \mathrm{m/s^2}$
