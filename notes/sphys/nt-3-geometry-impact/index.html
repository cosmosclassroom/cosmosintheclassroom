  • section: “Detailed Analysis” content: | ### Conservation of Momentum

    The Law of Conservation of Linear Momentum states that for a closed, isolated system (where the net external force is zero), the total momentum before a collision or interaction is equal to the total momentum after the interaction.

    #### Mathematical Expression \(\sum \vec{p}_{initial} = \sum \vec{p}_{final}\) \(m_1\vec{v}_{1i} + m_2\vec{v}_{2i} = m_1\vec{v}_{1f} + m_2\vec{v}_{2f}\)

    #### Key Points

    • Momentum ($\vec{p}$) is a vector quantity: $\vec{p} = m\vec{v}$
    • This law applies along each axis independently (x and y components)
    • Requires trigonometry to resolve vector components for angled impacts
    • Core principle in vehicle crash analysis and safety engineering

    #### Collision Types | Type | Condition | Energy Conservation | | :— | :— | :— | | Elastic | Objects separate after collision | Kinetic Energy Conserved | | Inelastic | Objects separate or deform | Kinetic Energy NOT Conserved | | Perfectly Inelastic | Objects stick together | Maximum KE lost to heat/sound/deformation |
