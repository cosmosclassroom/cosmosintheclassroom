Natural Disasters

Welcome to Natural Disasters, an interdisciplinary course that explores the physics, geology, and environmental science behind Earth’s most powerful natural phenomena. Students investigate the causes, effects, and mitigation strategies for major natural disasters.

Course Overview

This course emphasizes:

  • Earth System Science: Understanding interconnected Earth processes
  • Physics Applications: Wave mechanics, fluid dynamics, thermodynamics in natural systems
  • Risk Assessment: Quantitative analysis of hazard probability and impact
  • Mitigation Strategies: Engineering and policy approaches to disaster preparedness
  • Data Analysis: Interpreting real scientific data from monitoring systems

Course Units

Unit 1: Earthquakes

  • Core Topics: Seismic waves, tectonic plate mechanics, fault systems
  • Key Skills: Wave analysis, energy calculations, magnitude scales
  • Physics Focus: Elastic wave propagation, stress and strain, energy release
  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

Unit 2: Tsunamis

  • Core Topics: Water wave mechanics, wave generation and propagation
  • Key Skills: Wave equation applications, energy transfer, coastal impact analysis
  • Physics Focus: Fluid dynamics, wave interference, shallow water waves
  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

Unit 3: Wildfire

  • Core Topics: Combustion physics, heat transfer, atmospheric dynamics
  • Key Skills: Thermodynamics, heat transfer mechanisms, weather analysis
  • Physics Focus: Chemical energy, thermal radiation, fluid flow
  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

Unit 4: Flooding

  • Core Topics: Hydrodynamics, precipitation physics, watershed analysis
  • Key Skills: Fluid mechanics, statistical analysis, system modeling
  • Physics Focus: Pressure, flow dynamics, conservation of mass and energy
  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

Unit 5: Portfolio

  • Core Topics: Disaster risk assessment, mitigation planning, case studies
  • Key Skills: Data synthesis, risk quantification, communication
  • Physics Focus: Integration of multiple physical processes and systems
  • Content Types: Lectures Flexbook Labs Problem Sets Research Briefs

Scientific Approach

Natural Disasters integrates multiple scientific disciplines through:

  • Quantitative Analysis: Using physics and mathematics to understand disaster processes
  • Data Interpretation: Working with real seismographic, meteorological, and hydrological data
  • Systems Thinking: Understanding complex interactions between Earth systems
  • Risk Assessment: Probability analysis and uncertainty quantification
  • Mitigation Engineering: Physics-based approaches to disaster preparedness

Assessment Philosophy

Assessment emphasizes scientific reasoning through:

  • Data Analysis: Interpreting real scientific datasets and monitoring information
  • Physics Applications: Using physical principles to explain natural phenomena
  • Risk Evaluation: Quantitative assessment of hazard probability and impact
  • Communication: Presenting scientific findings to diverse audiences
  • Problem-Solving: Developing evidence-based mitigation strategies

Course Resources

  • Course Vocabulary - Key scientific terms and concepts
  • Real-time monitoring data from USGS, NOAA, and international agencies
  • Laboratory simulations and physical models
  • GIS and data analysis software tools

This course develops scientific literacy and quantitative reasoning skills while exploring some of Earth’s most powerful and impactful natural phenomena.
