About This Course

Natural Disasters explores the scientific principles behind earthquakes, volcanoes, hurricanes, floods, and other natural phenomena. You'll learn to analyze real data, evaluate risks, and understand how human actions can mitigate or exacerbate natural hazards.

What You'll Find Here:

  • Lectures: Scientific explanations of natural phenomena
  • Hexagon Labs: Real-world data analysis and case studies
  • Problem Sets: Applied problem-solving scenarios
  • Assessments: Understanding checks and evaluations
  • Flexbooks: Supplemental reading and resources
