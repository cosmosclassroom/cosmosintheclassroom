A prolonged heatwave has created dangerously dry conditions in the forests surrounding Branford. You are tasked with analyzing the weather data from the past week to determine which day posed the greatest fire risk.

To do this, you will use a simplified Fire Weather Index (FWI). This index combines multiple weather factors into a single number that represents the potential for a wildfire to start and spread.

**Simplified FWI Formula:**
$$ FWI = (Temperature [°C] - Relative \, Humidity [\%]) \times (Wind \, Speed [km/h] \div 10) $$

A higher FWI number indicates a more dangerous fire risk. Your analysis will be critical for the Engineer, who needs to know the worst-case conditions their firebreak designs must withstand. investigation:   title: "Investigation"   content: |
