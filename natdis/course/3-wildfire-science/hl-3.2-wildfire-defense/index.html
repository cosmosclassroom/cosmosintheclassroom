**Client Need:** The Branford Town Council requires a data-driven recommendation for a new building and landscaping code that can be applied to a typical quarter-acre residential lot.

**Success Criteria:**
- **Performance:** The chosen design must reduce a home's exposure to radiant heat from a wildfire by at least 75%.
- **Economic:** The initial implementation cost must be under $8,000 per household.
- **Feasibility:** The design must be achievable using standard landscaping practices. background:   title: "Background: Real-World Context"   content: |
**Case Study:** After the 2018 Camp Fire destroyed Paradise, California, investigations by the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS) proved that homes with a well-maintained defensible space of at least 30-100 feet had a dramatically higher survival rate.

**Engineering Approach:** Wildfire engineers treat defensible space as a system of zones. It is not just about clearing trees, but about strategically managing vegetation density and type to disrupt a fire's path and reduce its intensity as it nears a structure. investigation:   title: "Investigation: Design Analysis"   content: |

**Engineering Constraints:**
- **Performance:** Must achieve ≥75% radiant heat reduction.
- **Economic:** Budget limit of **$8,000** for initial setup.
- **Environmental:** Must minimize removal of mature, healthy trees.
