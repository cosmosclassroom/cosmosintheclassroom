**Stakeholders:** Your decision will affect homeowners, environmental groups, the local fire department, and real estate developers—all of whom have conflicting priorities.

**Timeline:** A vote is scheduled for the end of the month, before the peak of the wildfire season. background:   title: "Background: Real-World Context"   content: |
**Case Study:** In response to recurring, devastating wildfires, many towns in California and Colorado have enacted similar mandatory defensible space ordinances. For example, Boulder County, Colorado's "Defensible Space" code requires homeowners in wildfire-prone areas to meet specific vegetation clearance standards, which are verified by county inspectors.

**Political Reality:** These laws are often controversial. They have faced opposition from residents concerned about the cost of compliance, property rights, and the aesthetic impact of tree removal. Successful policies have required significant public outreach, financial assistance programs, and phased-in enforcement. information_synthesis:   title: "Information Synthesis"   content: |
