Q1: Fire Triangle (Core Concept)

  • id: q1 type: multiple_select prompt: “Select ALL three components that make up the ‘Fire Triangle’, which are necessary for a fire to start and continue:” points: 10 choices:
    • text: “Fuel (Combustible material)” value: “fuel” correct: true
    • text: “Moisture (Water content)” value: “moisture” correct: false
    • text: “Oxygen (Oxidizing agent)” value: “oxygen” correct: true
    • text: “Heat (Energy source)” value: “heat” correct: true explanation: “The three components of the fire triangle are Heat, Fuel, and Oxygen.”

Q2: Stability and Change (Climate Effect)

  • id: q2 type: short_answer prompt: “How does a prolonged drought directly influence the ‘Fuel’ component of the fire triangle?” points: 10 maxlength: 150 answer_keywords: [“drought”, “dry”, “moisture”, “decreases”] explanation: “Drought reduces the moisture content in trees and vegetation, making the fuel drier and thus much easier to ignite and burn quickly.”

Q3: Environmental Factors (Application of Lab 3.1)

  • id: q3 type: multiple_choice prompt: “Based on fire spread simulations, which topographical factor causes a wildfire’s Rate of Spread to increase the most dramatically?” points: 10 choices:
    • text: “A large area of flat ground” value: “flat” correct: false
    • text: “Fire moving downhill” value: “downhill” correct: false
    • text: “Fire moving uphill (steep slope)” value: “uphill” correct: true explanation: “Heat from a fire moving uphill preheats the unburned fuel ahead of it, significantly increasing the rate of spread.”

Q4: Calculation (Rate of Spread)

  • id: q4 type: numeric prompt: “A prescribed burn covers $120$ acres in $4$ hours. What is the average Rate of Spread in $\frac{\text{acres}}{\text{hour}}$?” points: 10 answer: 30 units: “acres/hour” tolerance: 0.01 explanation: “Rate of Spread = $\frac{Area \, Burned}{Time} = \frac{120 \, \text{acres}}{4 \, \text{hours}} = 30 \, \frac{\text{acres}}{\text{hour}}$”
