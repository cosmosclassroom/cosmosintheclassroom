| Upgrade | Cost (\$) | Risk Reduction Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A. Replace Wood Roof with Tile** | $15,000$ | $25$ |
| **B. Clear Defensible Space (30m)** | $3,000$ | $15$ |
| **C. Install Fire-Resistant Vents** | $2,000$ | $8$ |

What is the Cost-Effectiveness score (Risk Reduction Points per $\$1,000$) for **Upgrade B**?   points: 20   type: numeric   answer: 5.0   units: "points per $1,000"   tolerance: 0.01   explanation: "Upgrade B Cost in thousands is $\$3,000 \\rightarrow 3$. Cost-Effectiveness = $\\frac{15 \\, \\text{points}}{3 \\, \\text{thousand}} = 5.0 \\, \\frac{\\text{points}}{\\$1,000}$"

Justify your choice using the concept of Stability and Change (the Unit’s CCC) and referencing the importance of reducing ignition risk and vulnerability.” points: 50 rows: 15 explanation: “An effective answer should recommend Defensible Space (B) or Roof/Vent Upgrades (A or C).
