Study Guide: Unit 1 Performance Task

Earthquake Resilience in Branford, CT

What to Review

  • Science notebook: Hexagon Labs 1.1, 1.2, 1.3a or 1.3b, 1.4
  • Problem Set 1.1: Wave speed, travel time, intensity/magnitude
  • Engineering notes: Cross-bracing, base isolation, shake-table results
  • Policy notes: Cost-benefit analysis, community safety options

Key Concepts & Skills to Review

  • Calculate P-wave travel time using wave speed and distance
  • Assign Mercalli Intensity ratings from observed effects and vulnerability
  • Explain how distance, population, and infrastructure affect earthquake impact
  • Compare building designs and their response to shaking
  • Describe how base isolation and bracing improve safety
  • Interpret and justify policy choices using cost, benefit, and people helped
  • Write clear, concise explanations and recommendations

Assessment Format

  • Three roles: Scientist, Engineer, Policymaker
  • Question types: Calculations, selected responses, short written explanations
  • No model or presentation required: Just answer and explain your thinking

Tips for Success

  • Show your work for all calculations
  • Use evidence from your notebook and labs
  • Write in clear, simple sentences
  • Justify recommendations using data
  • Consider community needs and fairness
