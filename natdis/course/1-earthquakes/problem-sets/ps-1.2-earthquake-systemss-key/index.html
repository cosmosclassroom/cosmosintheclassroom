Problem Set 1.2: Systems & Solutions

Your Role: The Engineer
Your Mission: Use scientific data to evaluate engineering solutions and understand the trade-offs between safety, cost, and effectiveness in seismic design.

Building on Scientific Findings

From Previous Work: Scientists determined seismic wave patterns and local earthquake risks.

Your Engineering Challenge: Use this information to analyze structural design options and evaluate their performance.

Why This Matters: Engineers must translate scientific understanding into practical solutions that protect communities while managing limited resources.

Key Engineering Formulas

Risk assessment formula: \(Risk = Hazard \times Vulnerability\)

Cost-effectiveness ratio: \(Cost \, Effectiveness = \frac{Total \, Benefit}{Total \, Cost}\)

Factor of safety: \(Factor \, of \, Safety = \frac{Resisting \, Forces}{Driving \, Forces}\)

Part 1: Risk Analysis

1. A community has a seismic hazard rating of $7$ (on a scale of 1-10) and a vulnerability rating of $6$ (on a scale of 1-10).
Calculate the overall risk level for this community.

2. After implementing earthquake-resistant building codes, the same community reduces its vulnerability to $3$.
What is the new risk level? How much did the risk decrease?

Part 2: Design Trade-offs

3. Three building designs have the following characteristics:

Design Construction Cost Safety Factor Maintenance Cost/Year
A $2.5 million 1.8 $50,000
B $4.2 million 2.5 $30,000
C $6.0 million 3.2 $15,000

Which design offers the highest safety factor? Which has the lowest total cost over 10 years?

4. Using the cost-effectiveness formula, if Design B prevents $8 million in potential earthquake damage:
Calculate the cost-effectiveness ratio for Design B (including 10-year maintenance costs).

Part 3: Systems Analysis

5. An engineer must choose between retrofitting 50 older buildings at $$100{,}000$ each or building 10 new earthquake-resistant structures at $$500{,}000$ each.
Which option protects more buildings? Which option provides better long-term safety per dollar spent?

6. In 2-3 sentences, explain how engineering decisions must balance multiple systems (economic, safety, environmental) when designing for earthquake resilience.
