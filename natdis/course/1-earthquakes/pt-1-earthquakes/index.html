# Q5: Essay/Diagram - Describing Triangulation (Modeling/SEP)

  • id: q5triangulation_essay type: essay prompt: “Explain the process of triangulation used by seismologists to locate an earthquake’s epicenter. Your answer should specify the minimum number of seismic stations required and what information each station must provide.” points: 10 rows: 6 explanation: “Triangulation requires data from a minimum of three seismic stations. Each station measures the arrival time difference between the P-wave and the S-wave ($Time{S-P}$). This difference is used with a travel-time graph to determine the distance to the epicenter. A circle is then drawn around each station with this distance as the radius. The point where all three circles intersect is the epicenter of the earthquake.”
