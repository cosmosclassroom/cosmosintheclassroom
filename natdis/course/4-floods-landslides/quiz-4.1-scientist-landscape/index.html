Question 1: Multiple Choice (Scientist’s Role)

  • id: q1 type: multiple_choice prompt: “As a scientist reading a topographical map, you see an area where the contour lines are packed very close together. What does this tell you about the land?” points: 15 choices:
    • text: “The area is very flat.” value: “flat” correct: false
    • text: “The area has a very steep slope.” value: “steep” correct: true
    • text: “A river or stream is located there.” value: “river” correct: false
    • text: “The area is a wide, open floodplain.” value: “floodplain” correct: false explanation: “Contour lines show elevation. When they are close together, it means the elevation is changing very quickly over a short distance, which indicates a steep slope.”

Question 2: Multiple Choice (Vocabulary)

  • id: q2 type: multiple_choice prompt: “What is the correct term for an area of land that drains all the streams and rainfall to a common outlet, like a single river?” points: 15 choices:
    • text: “A floodplain” value: “floodplain” correct: false
    • text: “A retention pond” value: “retention” correct: false
    • text: “A watershed” value: “watershed” correct: true
    • text: “A factor of safety” value: “fos” correct: false explanation: “A watershed is the entire land area that channels rainfall and snowmelt to a single creek, stream, river, lake, or ocean.”

Question 3: Numeric Input (Engineer’s Role)

  • id: q3 type: numeric prompt: “An engineer studies a hillside where the forces resisting a landslide are 2,000 units, and the forces driving the landslide are 500 units. Using the formula, calculate the Factor of Safety. (Factor of Safety = Resisting Forces / Driving Forces)” points: 25 answer: 4 units: “” min: 3.5 max: 4.5 step: 0.1 explanation: “Using the formula: Factor of Safety = 2000 / 500 = 4. A factor of safety greater than 1 means the slope is stable.”

Question 4: Multiple Select (Engineer’s Role)

  • id: q4 type: multiple_select prompt: “A city planner needs to reduce the risk of flooding in a vulnerable area. Select ALL of the engineering solutions that could help manage excess water.” points: 25 choices:
    • text: “Building levees along the riverbank.” value: “levees” correct: true
    • text: “Creating a retention pond to hold rainwater.” value: “retention_pond” correct: true
    • text: “Asking residents to build taller houses.” value: “taller_houses” correct: false
    • text: “Replacing concrete sidewalks with permeable pavement.” value: “pavement” correct: true
    • text: “Zoning the area for parks instead of buildings.” value: “zoning” correct: false explanation: “Levees act as barriers to rising water. Retention ponds store excess water. Permeable pavement allows water to soak into the ground instead of running off. Zoning is a policy solution, not a direct engineering one.”

Question 5: Short Answer (Scientist’s Role)

  • id: q5 type: short_answer prompt: “In one or two sentences, explain how an extreme rainfall event can cause a landslide on an unstable slope.” points: 20 maxlength: 250 explanation: “Extreme rain can cause ‘soil saturation,’ where the ground becomes filled with water. This makes the soil heavier and weaker, reducing its grip and allowing gravity to more easily pull it down the slope.”
