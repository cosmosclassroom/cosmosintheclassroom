Q1: Cause and Effect (Tsunami Generation)

  • id: q1 type: multiple_choice prompt: “What type of earthquake movement is the primary cause of a devastating tsunami?” points: 10 choices:
    • text: “Horizontal (strike-slip) movement that causes land to slide past itself.” value: “horizontal” correct: false
    • text: “Vertical displacement (up or down) of the seafloor.” value: “vertical” correct: true
    • text: “Ground shaking that creates tiny ripples that combine into large waves.” value: “shaking” correct: false explanation: “A tsunami is created when a large volume of water is vertically displaced, typically by a fault snapping upward or downward at a subduction zone.”

Q2: Wave Mechanics (Speed)

  • id: q2 type: multiple_choice prompt: “The speed of a deep-ocean tsunami wave is primarily determined by what factor?” points: 10 choices:
    • text: “The wind speed and direction above the ocean surface.” value: “wind” correct: false
    • text: “The total energy released by the initial earthquake (Moment Magnitude).” value: “magnitude” correct: false
    • text: “The depth of the ocean floor.” value: “depth” correct: true explanation: “The speed of a shallow-water wave (which tsunamis are, even in the deep ocean) is calculated using the formula $Speed = \sqrt{g \cdot d}$, where $d$ is the depth. The deeper the water, the faster the wave.”

Q3: Shoaling (Key Concept)

  • id: q3 type: short_answer prompt: “What is the term for the process where a tsunami dramatically increases in height as it moves from the deep ocean into shallow coastal water?” points: 10 maxlength: 100 answer_keywords: [“shoaling”] explanation: “Shoaling is the process where a wave slows down and compresses as it hits shallower water, forcing its height to increase.”

Q4: Calculation (Travel Time)

  • id: q4 type: numeric prompt: “If a tsunami is traveling at $800 \, \frac{\text{km}}{\text{hr}}$, how many hours will it take to travel across an ocean basin that is $6,400 \, \text{km}$ wide?” points: 10 answer: 8 units: “hours” tolerance: 0.001 explanation: “Travel Time = $\frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}} = \frac{6,400 \, \text{km}}{800 \, \frac{\text{km}}{\text{hr}}} = 8$ hours.”
