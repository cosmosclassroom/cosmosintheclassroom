    Analysis Questions

    1. Risk vs. Disruption: The Scientist’s 12.75-hour prediction is an estimate. There is always a margin of error. Which policy option provides the largest safety buffer if the tsunami arrives earlier than expected? Which option causes the least disruption if the tsunami is weaker or later than expected?

    2. Communication: Look at the alert methods. Why would an Immediate Order use a broad tool like the Emergency Broadcast System, while a Phased Order might use more targeted alerts? What are the pros and cons of each method?
