Template Usage Instructions

  • Sentence starters provided in all reflection boxes

  • Explicit connections between parts reduce cognitive load
  • Evidence requirements are specific and manageable
  • Success criteria embedded throughout

Assessment Flexibility:

  • Progressive evaluation - each part can be assessed separately
  • Revision opportunities - students can improve earlier sections
  • Multiple formats - supports different learning styles
  • Clear rubric alignment - each section maps to specific NGSS practices

Teacher Implementation Notes:

  • Time management - each part designed for completion after corresponding unit work
  • Differentiation - extension questions can be added for advanced students
  • Portfolio development - document serves as cumulative assessment
  • Civic engagement - final reflection connects to community participation

Validation Checklist:

  • All eight NGSS practices explicitly assessed
  • Cross-cutting concepts integrated naturally throughout
  • Role progression clearly scaffolded and connected
  • Local (Branford) application meaningful and specific
  • System breakdown analysis addresses both technical and political factors
  • “From Space to Place” narrative coherent across all scales
  • Assessment points clearly marked for rubric development
  • Appropriate challenge level for less-motivated students
