The town of Branford has identified a 2,000-meter stretch of vulnerable coastline that needs immediate protection.

    You have been given a budget of $250,000,000. Your task is to evaluate three potential barrier designs. You must choose the design that provides the best protection without exceeding the budget. The work you do here will be handed to the Policymaker, who will need to approve the funding and justify the decision to the public. investigation: title: “Investigation” content: | Use the data below to analyze each option. Calculate the total cost for a 2,000-meter barrier and determine if it fits within the budget.

    Barrier Options Data Table | Barrier Design | Cost per Meter | Wave Height Reduction | Construction Time | | :— | :— | :— | :— | | Option A: Reinforced Seawall | $150,000 | 85% | 24 months | | Option B: Offshore Breakwater | $115,000 | 70% | 36 months | | Option C: Hybrid Living Shoreline | $95,000 | 55% | 18 months |

    Calculation Steps

    1. Calculate Total Cost for Each Option: Use the formula \(Total \, Cost = Cost \, per \, Meter \times 2,000 \, m\)
      • Option A Cost: \(\$150,000 \times 2,000 = \$300,000,000\)
      • Option B Cost: \(\$115,000 \times 2,000 = \$230,000,000\)
      • Option C Cost: \(\$95,000 \times 2,000 = \$190,000,000\)
    2. Analyze Constraints:
      • Budget Constraint: The total budget is $250,000,000. Which option(s) are over budget?

        Option A ($300M) is over budget. Options B ($230M) and C ($190M) are within budget.

      • Decision: Based on the budget, which is the most effective option you can afford?

        Option B (Offshore Breakwater) offers the highest wave height reduction (70%) while remaining within the budget. synthesis: title: “Synthesis and Communication” prompts: - title: “Justifying Your Design” content: “You have selected Option B.
