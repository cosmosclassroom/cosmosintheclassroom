Q1: The Scientist’s Foundation (Wave Dynamics)

  • id: q1 type: short_answer prompt: “Tsunamis dramatically increase in height as they approach the coast. This is caused by the wave slowing down when it encounters shallow water. What is the technical term for this effect?” points: 10 maxlength: 50 answer_keywords: [“shoaling”, “shoal”] explanation: “Shoaling is the process where a wave slows down and compresses as it hits shallower water, forcing its height to increase dramatically.”
