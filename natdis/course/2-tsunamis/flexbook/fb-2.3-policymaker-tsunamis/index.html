Template Usage Instructions

Role Voice Guidelines:

  • Scientist (Ch. 1): Voice of curiosity and precision. “What’s really happening here?” Focus on evidence and understanding.
  • Engineer (Ch. 2): Voice of problem-solving and innovation. “How can we build something better?” Focus on solutions and optimization.
  • Policymaker (Ch. 3): Voice of wisdom and responsibility. “How do we make good choices for everyone?” Focus on balance and community good.

Information Flow Architecture:

  • Chapter 1 → 2: Scientific findings become engineering design requirements
  • Chapter 2 → 3: Engineering options become policy choices
  • Chapter 3: Synthesizes both previous chapters for community decision

Validation Checklist:

  • Role voice consistent with position in 3-chapter sequence
  • Explicit connections to previous chapter(s)
  • Clear handoff information for next chapter
  • Cross-cutting concepts build cumulatively
  • Local (Branford) applications become more specific through sequence
  • System breakdown analysis addresses role-specific vulnerabilities
