  • “Interpret topographical maps to identify watersheds, floodplains, and steep slopes”
  • “Explain how the shape of the land (topography) dictates the flow of water and the risk of landslides”
  • “Define soil saturation and explain how it can trigger a landslide” keyVocabulary:
  • term: “Watershed” definition: “An area of land that drains all the streams and rainfall to a common outlet such as the outflow of a reservoir, mouth of a bay, or any point along a stream channel.”
  • term: “Stream Discharge” definition: “The volume of water moving down a stream or river per unit of time.”
  • term: “Slope” definition: “The steepness of a landform, calculated as the ratio of vertical change (rise) to horizontal change (run).” keyFormulae:
  • formula: | $Slope = \frac{Rise}{Run}$ description: | Slope: This formula calculates the steepness of a hillside. “Rise” is the change in elevation (from contour lines), and “Run” is the horizontal distance (from the map scale).
  • formula: | $Discharge = Area \cdot Velocity$ description: | Stream Discharge: This calculates the volume of water flowing in a river. It’s the cross-sectional area of the river channel (Width x Depth) multiplied by the average speed (velocity) of the water.
