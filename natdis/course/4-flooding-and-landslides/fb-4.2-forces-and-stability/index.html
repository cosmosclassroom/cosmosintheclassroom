  • “Explain the concept of ‘Factor of Safety’ by balancing driving forces vs. resisting forces”
  • “Conduct a simplified cost-benefit analysis of different mitigation strategies” keyVocabulary:
  • term: “Factor of Safety” definition: “A ratio used in engineering to assess the stability of a structure or slope, comparing the forces resisting failure to the forces driving failure.”
  • term: “Cost-Benefit Analysis” definition: “A systematic process used to compare the financial costs of a project against its benefits (e.g., property protected, lives saved).” keyFormulae:
  • formula: | $Factor \, of \, Safety = \frac{Resisting \, Forces}{Driving \, Forces}$ description: | Factor of Safety (FS): This is the key ratio for stability. If FS > 1, the slope or structure is stable. If FS < 1, it will fail. Engineers aim for an FS significantly higher than 1 (e.g., 1.5 or 2) to ensure safety.
  • formula: | $Pressure = \frac{Force}{Area}$ description: | Pressure: This defines how a force is distributed. The force of floodwater is spread over the area of a levee.
