Task | Concept Mapping Geo-Literacy Before we dive into the details of any specific disaster, we’re going to build the mental map of our course. This course is a story—a narrative about Systems, Risk, and Resilience. Your job is to make a concept map that shows how all the pieces fit together. Consider the summary below from the prior readings, and make a concept map illustrating their connections. Note that do are not required to add the definitions of these terms to your map - they are here for your reference.

Core Concepts

Interconnections: How parts of a system are linked.

Scale & Scope: How a problem’s size and reach change its impact.

Vulnerability & Risk: The difference between a natural hazard and a community’s susceptibility to it.

Resilience & Adaptation: The ability to recover and adjust to future risks.

Community Trust: The importance of shared belief and open communication.

Trade-offs & Constraints: The difficult choices made when resources are limited.

Civic Action: The power of citizens to create change.
